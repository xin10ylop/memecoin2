# Social / X (Twitter) signal extraction for memecoins: data sources, costs, ingestion designs, and what actually predicts a runner (incl. Telegram call-channel front-running quantified)

## Executive summary

The single most important structural fact, which I measured live on 2026-08-23 against pump.fun's public API: the median lag from a source tweet being posted to the first token minted against that tweet is 11 seconds (p25 = 2s, p10 = 0s, p75 = 68s, p90 = 244s, n=63 clusters). Any pipeline slower than ~10s is not "early", it is late. X's own filtered stream has ~6-7s P99 delivery latency per X's docs, which puts the official firehose roughly at the median minter's speed, not ahead of it. Second: the X→token mapping is many-to-one and heavily spammed. Of 989 pump.fun tokens minted in one 40-minute window, 69.3% declared an X link and 79.2% of those links were a specific tweet permalink (/status/), not a profile; 67.5% of tweet-linked tokens shared their source tweet with at least one other token, and one tweet spawned 74 clone mints. So the job is not "find the viral tweet" — dozens of bots already found it — the job is picking which mint wins, and there the first-minted clone had the highest ATH in 73.9% of clusters that showed real dispersion (n=23). Third, and counterintuitive: contemporaneous tweet engagement does not predict the winner. Spearman(tweet likes, best-clone ATH) = -0.18 and Spearman(replies, ATH) ≈ 0.00, while Spearman(clone count, ATH) = +0.37. The canonical case: @InfraV2 posted a 15-like tweet at 15:25:07 UTC; the winning "GhostRider" token was minted at 15:25:45 (38 seconds later), peaked at $50.6K FDV at 15:53:25, and 70 later clones minted at t+23 to t+27 minutes all died at ~$2.7K (their initial market cap, i.e. zero net buying). On Telegram: t.me/s/<channel> is a free, no-auth, near-real-time (<60s) HTML ingestion path for public channels — no phone number, no Telethon, no ban risk — but contract addresses live in hrefs (solscan.io/account/<CA> and bot deeplinks), never in message text. I reconstructed 143 entry calls from a 108K-subscriber Solana channel (@solearlytrending) over 13 hours: the median token was 13.9 minutes old at call time, the median ATH/call-MC multiple was only 1.47x, the median time from call to ATH was 33 seconds, 43.4% of tokens had already made their all-time-high BEFORE the call landed, and 96.5% were below the call price at measurement with a median of -94.3%. A naive "buy the call, take profit at +25%" strategy hits 47.6% of the time and therefore requires the average loser to be capped at -22.7% to break even before fees — impossible against a median outcome of -94%. That channel's own self-reported scorecard is internally inconsistent (it claims 11 alerts hit 10X but 34 hit 15X+ in the same 24h), which is proof that self-reported channel stats must be independently reconstructed. On pricing: X killed flat tiers for new signups in Feb 2026; pay-per-use is $0.005/post read, $0.010/user read, $0.015/post create ($0.20 with a link), with filtered stream included (1,000 rules, 1 connection) and a monthly read cap that X's own docs state as 3M while every secondary source says 2M — sources disagree, verify in console before budgeting. twitterapi.io is ~33x cheaper at $0.15/1K tweets with a filter-rule webhook supporting interval_seconds down to 0.05, which is the only sub-second-polling commercial option I could verify. Two free primitives are worth more than most paid tiers here: cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result returns tweet timestamp, likes, reply count and the author's numeric ID with no auth at all, and X user IDs above ~1.29e18 are snowflake-encoded so account creation date decodes locally as ((id>>22)+1288834974657)/1000 — a zero-cost account-age check for the repurposed/hacked-account red flag.

## Numeric parameters

- **MAX_TWEET_AGE_AT_ENTRY_SEC** = `60 (hard reject above 600)` — Median tweet→first-mint lag is 11s and p90 is 244s. The verified GhostRider winner was minted at t+38s; all 70 clones minted at t+1382s to t+1609s died at their initial market cap with zero net buying.
- **END_TO_END_LATENCY_SLO_SEC** = `5.0 (alert at 8, kill-switch at 15)` — p25 of the field mints within 2s and p50 within 11s. X's own filtered stream is 6-7s P99, so anything built on it is already at median. Sub-budget: stream ≤1.5s, CA resolve ≤0.5s, risk ≤1.0s, tx land ≤2.0s.
- **CLONE_RANK_MAX** = `1 (reject any mint that is not the first referencing its tweet_id, unless all earlier mints have ath_market_cap < 1.5x their initial mcap)` — First-minted clone had the highest ATH in 73.9% of clusters with real dispersion (n=23); 62.5% unfiltered (n=40); median normalized winner rank = 0.0.
- **TWEET_CLUSTER_DEDUPE_TTL_SEC** = `21600 (6h), with a max of 1 open position per tweet_id` — 67.5% of tweet-linked tokens share their tweet with ≥1 other token; the worst case was 74 mints off one tweet. Without a per-tweet cap, one meta consumes the whole bankroll on 74 identical losers.
- **CLONE_VELOCITY_WINDOW_SEC / MIN_CLONES** = `120s window; treat ≥2 distinct mints on one tweet_id inside 120s as a positive crowding signal` — Clone count was the ONLY positively-correlated feature found: Spearman(clone count, best-clone ATH) = +0.369, vs likes -0.179 and replies -0.008.
- **MIN_TWEET_LIKES** = `DO NOT USE as a gate` — Spearman(favorite_count, best-clone ATH) = -0.179 (negative). Winners at $139,980 / $50,785 / $18,127 / $11,211 ATH came from tweets with 35 / 17 / 1 / 0 likes respectively.
- **MIN_AUTHOR_ACCOUNT_AGE_DAYS** = `30 (reject below); flag accounts with id < 1.29e18 (pre-~2021) for repurposed-account review` — >56% of Twitter accounts in crypto-manipulation datasets are bots or suspended (arXiv 2001.10289). Age is computable free via ((user_id>>22)+1288834974657)/1000; verified against @InfraV2 id 1633563551905218561 → 2023-03-08.
- **MAX_TOKEN_AGE_AT_TG_CALL_ENTRY_SEC** = `60 (and prefer: never enter on a public call at all)` — Median called token was 13.9 min old; 43.4% had already made their ATH before the call landed; median time from call to ATH was 33 seconds.
- **TG_CALL_TIME_STOP_SEC** = `90` — Median call→ATH is 0.55 min (33s), p90 is 20.2 min. Anything not up within 90s is in the 96.5% that end below the call price.
- **TG_CALL_TAKE_PROFIT** = `+20% to +25%` — Post-call ATH reached ≥1.2x in 51.0% and ≥1.3x in 46.2% of all 143 calls (upper bound). Above +50% the hit rate collapses: ≥1.5x = 30.8%, ≥2.0x = 17.5%, ≥3.0x = 4.9%.
- **TG_CALL_BREAKEVEN_MAX_AVG_LOSS** = `22.7% at TP=+25% (16.5% at +15%, 25.8% at +40%, 21.0% at +60%, 21.2% at +100%)` — Identity: max_avg_loss = hit_rate * tp / (1 - hit_rate), with hit rates 47.6% / 52.4% / 39.2% / 25.9% / 17.5%. Median realized outcome is -94.3%, so no achievable stop meets this. Encode the identity and let the risk engine refuse the strategy.
- **CHANNEL_MAX_ALREADY_PEAKED_RATE** = `0.25 (drop any channel above it)` — @solearlytrending, 108K subs, scored 43.4% of calls where ATH preceded the call. A channel above ~25% is structurally selling you the top.
- **TOP10_NON_LP_HOLDER_MAX_PCT** = `20% conservative / 30% permissive` — MELT (arXiv 2602.13480v2) measures mean coordinated-account supply at 36.5% across 41k+ launches, so a 25-30% threshold sits at or below the population mean and will be permissive. 20% is the tighter setting.
- **DEMAND_METRIC** = `net SOL inflow and unique-funder count — NOT buyer count or tx count` — arXiv 2607.02795v3: 1,012 persistent sniper rings produce +16.1% first-30-min buyer-count lift with only +6.3% SOL inflow, not statistically significant. Buyer count is the metric they fake.
- **RUG_CLASSIFIER_WINDOW_SEC** = `300 (first 5 minutes of trading)` — arXiv 2608.20271v1: XGBoost on the first 5 minutes of trading data over 6.4M tokens achieves robust rug detection; most rugs show their characteristics within one hour.
- **RUSH_ORDER_BUCKET_SEC** = `25 (with a 5s bucket as the fast/low-recall variant)` — La Morgia et al.: 25s chunks + 7h window → F1 94.5%; dropping to 5s chunks cut Random Forest recall from 91.2% to 72.9%. Rush orders were the highest-Gini feature.
- **INITIAL_MCAP_ABOVE_PLATFORM_DEFAULT** = `prefer > 30 SOL default` — arXiv 2607.02823v3: strongest single graduation predictor, Cox HR 4.51; top-quartile initial-mcap tokens graduated at 0.634% vs 0.198% pooled.
- **TELEGRAM_PRESENT_WEIGHT** = `large positive weight, but expect it to fire on <2% of launches` — arXiv 2607.02823v3 reports 8.94x graduation lift (1.485% vs 0.166%, Cox HR 5.40 [4.73,6.17]), but I measured only 1.6% of new launches declaring a telegram field on 2026-08-23. High precision, near-zero recall.
- **TWITTERAPI_IO_RULE_INTERVAL_SEC** = `0.05 tier-1 KOLs / 1-5 tier-2 / 60 tier-3` — Documented range is 0.05 to 86400, default 60. Rule `value` is capped at 255 chars → ~15-20 handles per rule as `from:a OR from:b`. Must call update_rule with is_effect=1 or the rule never activates.
- **TME_POLL_INTERVAL_SEC** = `2-5 per channel` — t.me/s/ serves 20 posts/page and showed a message ~60s after posting. At 5s polling with a last-seen msg_id cursor you will not miss messages on channels posting ≤18.5 msg/hour (measured rate for @solearlytrending).
- **DEXSCREENER_POLL_INTERVAL_SEC** = `30 for /community-takeovers/latest/v1, 60 for /metas/trending/v1` — Published limit is 60 req/min on these endpoints; 30s and 60s polling for two endpoints uses 3 of 60 req/min, leaving headroom for token lookups.
- **EXPECTED_MINT_RATE** = `~25 pump.fun mints/minute (~1,495/hour); ~17 of those per minute carry an X link` — Measured 998 unique mints in a 40.05-minute window on 2026-08-23, 69.3% with a twitter field. Size your metadata_uri fetcher and IPFS gateway for ~25 req/s peak.
- **ATH_MCAP_DISTRIBUTION_PRIOR** = `p50 $3,188 / p75 $4,718 / p90 $7,298 / p95 $13,617 / p99 $154,335 / max $58.5M` — n=1,000 mints, 2026-08-23, from pump.fun ath_market_cap. The distribution is so heavy-tailed that any mean-based sizing is wrong; size on quantiles and expect the p99 to carry the P&L.

## Traps and failure modes

- THE EXIT-LIQUIDITY TRAP, MEASURED: 43.4% of tokens called by a 108K-subscriber Solana channel had ALREADY made their all-time high before the message landed. Median time from call to ATH among the rest was 33 seconds. Median outcome at measurement was -94.3% from the call price, with 96.5% below the call and 88.1% below half the call. If your architecture's happy path is 'read call → buy', you have built an exit-liquidity provider.
- SELF-REPORTED CHANNEL STATS ARE FABRICATED OR INCOHERENT: @solearlytrending's own 24h card claims 209 entry signals, a 46% '+50% win rate', '4.7X wins avg profit', and simultaneously 11 alerts at 10X but 34 at 15X+ — arithmetically impossible under any nesting. Across ~398 scraped messages only 4 mentioned 'down' or 'rug'. Survivorship is total: they post 'is up 2.1X' follow-ups and never post the -94% ones. Never ingest a channel's claimed performance as a feature; reconstruct it yourself from (call_ts, CA) joined to on-chain ATH.
- ENGAGEMENT-AT-DISCOVERY IS A NEGATIVE SIGNAL, NOT A POSITIVE ONE: Spearman(tweet likes, best-clone ATH) = -0.179. Building a 'trending tweet' filter that waits for likes to accumulate guarantees you arrive after the p90 of minters (244s). The tweet that produced the day's clean $50.6K winner had 15 likes.

### Buying Telegram calls needs a ≤22.7% average loss on losers to break even — against a median outcome of -94%. It is structurally unprofitable.

From the same 143-call sample, counting only ATHs that occurred AFTER the call (upper bound, since the 43.4% pre-peak group might in principle re-peak): reached ≥1.1x = 54.5% of all calls, ≥1.2x = 51.0%, ≥1.3x = 46.2%, ≥1.5x = 30.8%, ≥2.0x = 17.5%, ≥3.0x = 4.9%, ≥5.0x = 0.7%. Breakeven required average loss on non-winners: TP +15% needs ≤16.5%; TP +25% needs ≤22.7%; TP +40% needs ≤25.8%; TP +60% needs ≤21.0%; TP +100% needs ≤21.2%. Under a -50% assumed stop, every TP level is negative EV before fees: +15%→-15.9%/trade, +25%→-14.3%, +40%→-14.8%, +60%→-21.5%, +100%→-23.8%. And these hit rates are OPTIMISTIC — they assume you fill exactly at the quoted call MC with zero slippage, on a token whose median time-to-peak is 33 seconds.

**Actionable:** Encode the breakeven identity directly in your risk engine: required_max_avg_loss = hit_rate * tp / (1 - hit_rate). Refuse to enable any call-following strategy whose live measured stop-fill (not intended stop) exceeds that number. Given Solana memecoin slippage on a dumping book, a reliably-filled -20% stop is fiction; assume -40% to -60% realized and conclude the strategy is dead. Spend the engineering budget on the tweet→mint race instead.

### The classic Telegram pump-and-dump literature says the entire event resolves in ~5 minutes and insiders are tiered by 1-10 seconds — the memecoin version is the same mechanism, faster.

La Morgia et al., 'The Doge of Wall Street' (arXiv 2105.00733v2), full PDF extracted: Big Pump Signal (peak 200,000 Telegram + 250,000 Discord members) ran 41 pumps moving $129,674,881 'within an average of 5 minutes from their start' ($343,433,660 across full operation windows). They require opposite hold times; misclassifying one as the other is how you give back every gain. The 1-10 second insider tiering is the mathematical statement of why following a public call cannot work.
