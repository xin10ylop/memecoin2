# Smart-money wallet tracking and copy-trading as an alpha source (Solana memecoins, secondarily Base/BSC)

## Executive summary

Copy-trading is best modeled as a *screening* problem with a brutal false-discovery rate, not a "find the good wallet" problem. Base rate: over a 90-day window only 6.25% of 304,161 active Solana meme wallets were net profitable, median trader -$120, and 88% of the profitable ones made <$100 — so any naive "profitable wallet" filter is >90% noise. Pure-luck math makes this concrete: with a coin-flip null, ~17,300 of 300k wallets clear "≥20 trades and ≥70% win rate" by chance alone; you need ≥50 closed positions before a 70% win-rate screen has a defensible luck-tail (p≈0.0033), and ≥100 before an 70% screen is near-impossible by luck. Win rate alone is close to worthless without the payoff ratio — always pair it with median/mean ROI per closed position and a bootstrap on the actual PnL series. The strongest published persistence evidence is NOT from Solana: on Hyperliquid perps, wallet informativeness (10s markouts) has Spearman ρ=0.52 between adjacent 10-day windows with a reliability ceiling of 0.68 — i.e. real but noisy persistence, and the informational edge itself decays from +18.7% R² gain at 200ms to +3.0% at 30s. On Polymarket, only 3.14% of accounts are skilled out-of-sample, 44% of in-sample-skilled stay skilled in a holdout (vs ~10% for mutual funds), and ~60% of "lucky winners" revert to losses. Read across: assume a 10-14 day re-scoring cadence, expect roughly half of your selected wallets to survive each re-score, and treat any wallet whose edge you cannot re-verify inside 2-4 weeks as dead. On the mechanics: compute PnL yourself with per-(wallet,mint) FIFO lots over decoded DEX swaps only, denominated in SOL and USD, with SOL/stables treated as quote (never as positions), token transfers in/out excluded from cost basis, fees+priority fees+Jito tips subtracted, and a hard "sells > buys" invalid-lot guard (this is exactly what Solana Tracker's `pnlMode=strict` does). On separating skill from insiders/snipers: exclude wallets tagged `developer`/`pool` for the token, exclude any wallet appearing in the first-10 buyer ranks or in a Jito bundle with the deployer, and exclude sub-5-minute median hold times — MELT finds 36.5% of supply is held by bundle-linked coordinated accounts on the average pump.fun launch, so "early buyer" is usually insider, not alpha. On latency: Solana slots are 400ms; a co-located Rust bot on Yellowstone gRPC + ShredStream with Helius Sender/Jito plausibly lands same-slot or +1 slot, with reported 200-300ms typical landing (vendor-reported, unaudited); a websocket/RPC-polling stack is 400ms-30s behind and is not viable. The cost of that window is computable exactly on pump.fun: entry price ratio = ((30 + X)/30)² where X = SOL committed ahead of you, so 2 SOL ahead = +13.8%, 5 SOL = +36.1%, 10 SOL = +77.8%. Post-graduation on a 79-SOL pool the same 2 SOL is only +5.1%, which is the single strongest argument for copy-trading graduated/large-cap memecoins rather than bonding-curve launches. On the N-of-M question: the only rigorous causal study (166,098 pump.fun launches, 1.58M buyer events) finds that "≥2 distinct wallets from a persistent 1,012-cohort catalogue in the first-10 buyers" produces only a +16.1% lift in first-30-min non-cohort buyer count after propensity matching and contamination adjustment, and the SOL-inflow lift (+6.3%) is not distinguishable from zero — the naive +130.9% is ~half arithmetic self-flow and ~half launch-quality selection. Practitioner defaults that exist in shipped code are min_kols=3, window_minutes=15, coordination score ≥60; one tracker reports 9,404 such cluster events in 30 days at an average of 5.1 KOLs per cluster. Recommendation: use N≥3 distinct *independently-vetted, non-cofunded* smart wallets within 10-15 minutes as a signal, but be aware the published effect size for co-firing snipers is small and the confluence signal is heavily contaminated by cofunded sybils — dedupe by funding graph before counting N, or you are counting one entity as three.

## Numeric parameters

- **WALLET_MIN_CLOSED_POSITIONS** = `50` — Coin-flip null: at n=50 a ≥70% win rate has p=0.0033 (~990 lucky wallets out of 300k). At n=20 (Solana Tracker's minTrades default) p=0.058 → ~17,300 lucky wallets. 50 is the point where a win-rate screen starts to mean something; 100 is safer.
- **WALLET_MIN_WIN_RATE** = `0.55 floor, 0.70 ceiling (flag >0.70 as suspicious)` — Median win rate across 1,058 actively tracked Solana wallets is 57.1%, average 63.3%. Practitioner consensus: >70% over many trades indicates bots, wash trading or team wallets with front-running.
- **WALLET_MIN_PAYOFF_RATIO** = `1.5` — (win_rate·avg_win)/((1-win_rate)·avg_loss). Win rate alone is uninformative because memecoin payoffs are heavy-tailed; this is the only way to separate a 57%-WR positive-expectancy trader from a 70%-WR scalper bleeding on tails.
- **WALLET_MIN_REALIZED_PNL_USD (scoring window)** = `10000` — Only ~0.41% of the 13.55M pump.fun wallets ever cleared $10k realized; 88% of profitable 90-day wallets made under $100. This single filter removes ~99.6% of the universe.
- **WALLET_MIN_ACTIVE_DAYS** = `10 (of a 30-day window)` — Solana Tracker's minDays default is 3, which admits one-lucky-day wallets. 10 forces the PnL to be spread across the window.
- **MAX_SINGLE_TOKEN_PNL_SHARE** = `0.25` — Direct analogue of Solana Tracker's maxSingleTokenPct 'filters out one-hit-wonders'. A wallet whose 90-day PnL is >25% one token has not demonstrated repeatable process.
- **WALLET_MIN_MEDIAN_HOLD_SECONDS** = `300` — Solana Tracker documents hold time <5 minutes as 'likely automated or MEV-style behavior'. Sub-300s wallets are uncopyable at your latency regardless of their PnL.
- **WALLET_MAX_FIRST10_BUYER_APPEARANCES_PER_WEEK** = `2` — 'Multiple first-buyer appearances = same wallet sniping many launches'. The rigorous cohort study's premium tier is ≥20 launches in 13 days (~1.5/day); 2/week is a conservative sniper cutoff.
- **WALLET_RESCORE_INTERVAL_DAYS** = `7 (hard requalify every 14)` — The only measured persistence figure (Hyperliquid, ρ=0.52) is on adjacent 10-day windows. Polymarket: only 44% of skilled wallets stay skilled out-of-sample. Practitioner consensus is 'every couple of weeks'.
- **WALLET_MAX_AGE_OF_EDGE_DAYS** = `56` — No source supports an edge surviving 8 weeks unverified; 60% of lucky winners revert on the next sample. Retire any wallet not requalified inside 8 weeks.
- **PERMUTATION_TEST_ITERATIONS / THRESHOLD** = `10000 iterations, keep p < 0.01` — Directly mirrors the Polymarket study's 10,000-simulation coin-flip null used to separate skill from luck.
- **CONFLUENCE_MIN_DISTINCT_WALLETS (N)** = `3 (after funding-graph dedup)` — MadeOnSol production default min_kols=3 (range 2-50). The academic treatment definition is ≥2 distinct cohort wallets. 3 balances event rate against noise; dedup is mandatory because cofunded sybils otherwise inflate N.
- **CONFLUENCE_WINDOW_MINUTES** = `15 (test 10)` — MadeOnSol default window_minutes=15, range 1-60. Reported effect: '5+ KOLs within 15 minutes' produces statistically different short-term price action. 10 min is a tighter, higher-precision variant worth A/B testing.
- **CONFLUENCE_MIN_SCORE** = `60/100` — MadeOnSol min_score default 60; observed average cluster score is 67 with 5.1 KOLs, so 60 sits just below the observed mean.
- **EXPECTED_CONFLUENCE_EVENT_RATE** = `~313/day at N≥3 (9,404 per 30 days)` — Measured on a 1,058-wallet tracked universe. Use it to size your downstream filtering: you cannot trade 313 signals/day, so confluence must be a gate, not a trigger.
- **MAX_TOP10_HOLDER_CONCENTRATION (post-cluster-collapse)** = `0.25 (vendor Danger threshold is 0.15)` — Solana Tracker scores 'top 10 holders own more than 15%' as Danger. MELT shows raw top-10 understates true concentration by ~24pp on high-risk tokens once bundle-linked accounts are merged, so collapse clusters BEFORE applying the threshold.
- **MAX_SNIPER_SUPPLY_PCT / MAX_INSIDER_SUPPLY_PCT / MAX_BUNDLER_SUPPLY_PCT** = `0.10 / 0.10 / 0.05` — Exact Warning-level thresholds in Solana Tracker's published risk weights (snipers >10% = 3000, insiders >10% = 3000, bundlers >5% = 3000).
- **MAX_CLUSTER_SUPPLY_PCT (funding-graph component)** = `0.20` — Stated practitioner threshold: '20%+ of a token's supply controlled by a connected wallet cluster' = significantly elevated risk. MELT's population average of bundle-coordinated supply is 36.5%, so 20% is already lenient.
- **FUNDING_GRAPH_DEPTH** = `3 hops` — Documented insider topology is source → 5-10 intermediaries → each funds 3-5 buyers = 15-50 wallets at 2-3 hops. Helius funded-by only returns the FIRST funder (depth 1), so depth-1 clustering catches roughly 1/5 of a tree.
- **CO_OCCURRENCE_EDGE_WEIGHT_MIN** = `3` — The published sniper-cohort detector retains co-occurrence edges only at weight ≥3 ('the qualifying pair threshold'), explicitly as a defence against accidental co-occurrence given 166,098 launches and 5-15 first-rank buyers each.
- **COHORT_SCORE_FORMULA** = `10·(#launches touched) + 5/mean_first_rank + sqrt(total SOL committed)` — Published scoring function; median cohort score 52.8, max 430.44, high-tier at ≥10 launches OR score ≥100. Reuse as a sniper-ness feature for exclusion.
- **COORDINATED_EXIT_TRIGGER** = `≥3 wallets from one funding component sell the same mint within 300-600 seconds` — Documented insider dump pattern: 'within a 5-10 minute window, they all sell simultaneously'.
- **PUMPFUN_ENTRY_SLIPPAGE_FORMULA** = `((30 + X_sol_ahead) / 30)^2 - 1` — Derived from verified pump.fun constants (30 SOL virtual SOL reserve, 1.073e9 virtual token reserve, constant product). Gives +6.8% at 1 SOL, +13.8% at 2, +36.1% at 5, +77.8% at 10 SOL of front-running flow. Full curve to graduation (~85 SOL) is only 14.69x total.
- **AMM_ENTRY_SLIPPAGE_FORMULA** = `((R_sol + X_sol_ahead) / R_sol)^2 - 1` — Same constant-product math with real reserves. 79 SOL graduation pool: 2 SOL ahead = +5.13%. 300 SOL pool: +1.34%. 1,000 SOL pool at 5 SOL ahead: +1.00%. This is the quantitative case for copy-trading liquid tokens only.
- **MAX_ACCEPTABLE_COPY_SLIPPAGE** = `8% on bonding curve, 3% post-graduation` — Above these the ((R+X)/R)^2 math says you are buying the target's exit. Abort the copy rather than chase.
- **MIN_POOL_SOL_RESERVE_FOR_COPY** = `300 SOL` — At R=300, 2 SOL of front-running costs 1.34% — inside a tolerable band. Below ~150 SOL the latency tax dominates any plausible wallet edge.
- **DETECT_TO_SUBMIT_BUDGET_MS** = `60 (p99 signal-to-landed target 80)` — Stated competitive budget for Solana copy bots; the 400ms slot means missing it drops you to slot+1 or later.
- **EXPECTED_LANDING_LATENCY_MS** = `200-300 typical, 15 best case (vendor-reported, unaudited)` — Co-located Rust bot in Frankfurt on Yellowstone gRPC + Jito ShredStream with Helius Sender/bloXroute/QuickNode Fastlane; reported same-slot or +1-slot landing vs the target wallet.
- **INGEST_PATH_LATENCY** = `Yellowstone gRPC 10-40ms; ShredStream 50-200ms earlier than Turbine; WebSocket 400ms-30s; polling 300ms+` — WebSocket figure is documented by Helius (post-shred-reassembly at processed/confirmed/finalized commitment). The gRPC and ShredStream figures are vendor-reported.
- **JITO_MIN_TIP / SWQOS_MIN_TIP** = `0.0002 SOL / 5,000 lamports (0.000005 SOL)` — Documented Helius Sender minimums. Competitive searcher bundles reportedly tip 50-70% of expected profit; retail Telegram-bot users reportedly tip ~0.1 SOL/tx during hype.
- **COMPUTE_UNIT_LIMIT_DEFAULT** = `200,000` — Helius Sender default; set explicitly per-transaction via ComputeBudget to avoid overpaying priority fees.
- **UNSCORABLE_POSITION_GUARD** = `drop any (wallet,mint) where cumulative sold_qty > cumulative bought_qty` — This is exactly Solana Tracker's pnlMode=strict rule; it catches airdropped/transferred-in/minted supply that has no on-DEX cost basis and would otherwise show as infinite ROI.
- **WASH_TRADE_FLAGS** = `single_token_volume_share > 0.40 AND median round-trip < 600s; OR counterparty in same funding component; OR N wallets funded with identical SOL amounts from one source` — MELT measures 21.4% of pre-migration transactions as wash trades. Documented case: 200 bots each seeded with exactly 0.5 SOL faked >$500k volume in 12 hours.
- **POST_MIGRATION_STOP_LOSS / HORIZON** = `tighter than -40%, re-evaluate within 20 minutes of migration` — MELT: 73% of migrated tokens fall below 40% of migration price and 60.26% below 20%, within the 20 minutes after migration.
- **DEPLOYER_REPUTATION_GATE** = `deployer historical bonding rate ≥ 40% over ≥5 launches` — Definition of the 'elite' deployer tier; these achieve a 71% bonding rate against a 1-2% overall graduation baseline — a 35-70x lift and an orthogonal alpha source to wallet copying.
- **EXPECTED_FOLLOWER_CAPTURE_RATE** = `40-55% of the leader's gross return` — Reported leader 5-15%/mo vs follower 2-8%/mo after slippage, delayed execution and fees. Low confidence — calibrate from your own fills within the first 200 copies.
- **WALLET_UNIVERSE_SURVIVAL_PRIOR** = `~44% of top-decile wallets survive one month` — Polymarket out-of-sample skilled-retention rate. Qualify ~2.3x as many wallets as you intend to run live.

## Traps and failure modes

- Routed-swap double counting: dex_solana.trades and naive Jupiter parsing emit one row per hop, so a 3-pool route becomes 3 trades. This inflates volume ~3x and manufactures phantom round-trips that corrupt both win rate and hold-time. Dedupe by transaction signature and net token delta.
- Unrealized PnL marked at last-trade price. Every 'top trader' leaderboard is polluted by wallets holding illiquid bags marked at a price no one will pay. Always mark remaining size by simulating a sell of that exact quantity against live reserves. Rank on REALIZED PnL only.
- Airdrops, transfers-in and mints create sells without buys, producing infinite ROI. This is the single most common source of fake smart-money wallets. Enforce the sells>buys guard (pnlMode=strict semantics) and mark such (wallet,mint) pairs unscorable rather than zero-cost-basis.
- Counting cofunded sybils as independent confluence. A funding tree of 5-10 intermediaries each funding 3-5 buyers gives you 15-50 addresses that will trivially trip any N≥3 confluence rule as if they were three independent smart-money opinions. Union-find on the funding graph to depth 3 BEFORE counting N, and merge on shared Jito bundle ID.
- Helius funded-by only returns the FIRST SOL funder. Depth-1 clustering on it catches roughly one-fifth of a standard 2-3 hop funding tree and will give you false confidence that wallets are independent.
- Copying block-0 / bonding-curve entries. Structurally impossible: the ((30+X)/30)^2 curve means 5 SOL of flow landing ahead of you costs 36%, 10 SOL costs 78%. Wallets whose PnL comes from first-10-buyer ranks are uncopyable by construction; detect them by median first-buyer rank ≤10 and by first-buyer appearances per week.
- Selection bias in leaderboards. Only 12% of top-profit Polymarket accounts were actually skilled; ranking by raw PnL selects for size and luck, not process. Rank by a luck-adjusted, single-token-capped, fee-inclusive statistic.
- Survivorship and regime contamination. Pump.fun's profitable-wallet share swung from 30.1% (Jun 2025) to 73.3% (Apr 2026). A wallet scored in a bull window will look skilled purely from beta. Score every wallet relative to the median active wallet in the same window.
- Sniper cohort co-firing is a much weaker signal than it looks. The naive lift from '≥2 persistent-cohort wallets in the first-10 buyers' is +130.9%, but half is arithmetic self-flow and most of the rest is launch-quality selection; the residual coordination-specific effect on buyer count is only +16.1% and on SOL inflow is statistically zero. 7.0% of cohort-touched launches attract ZERO other buyers in the first 30 minutes.
- Fee blindness. Base fee + priority fee (compute_unit_price x CUs consumed) + Jito tip + LP fee + platform fee can exceed the entire edge on a high-frequency wallet. A PnL engine that ignores fees systematically ranks the highest-frequency (worst-net) wallets highest.
- Mixing PnL methodologies. Dune uses average cost basis; Solana Tracker does not document its method; Cielo documents nothing. Cross-source rankings are meaningless. Recompute every candidate in one engine (recommend FIFO) before comparing.
- pnlMode is not supported everywhere. KOL leaderboards, history, chart, performance, highlights, risk, token-traders, first-buyers and wallet-summary-batch all return a fixed server-side view. Comparing a strict-mode wallet score against a fixed-view leaderboard rank is an apples-to-oranges bug.
- Cielo silently drops Solana wallets exceeding 3,000 tx/day, so your fastest candidates are invisible there. Never conclude 'this wallet has no activity' from a Cielo miss.
- Birdeye caps pagination at offset+limit ≤ 10,000, so a single leaderboard query can never reach more than 10k wallets. Any 'full universe' claim built on one Birdeye query is wrong.
- Arkham throttles /transfers, /swaps and /counterparties to 1 req/sec. Attempting funding-graph construction in the hot path will stall your bot; build it offline.
- Wash-traded and self-funded 'bait' wallets. 21.4% of pump.fun pre-migration transactions are wash trades; a documented case seeded 200 bots with exactly 0.5 SOL each to fake >$500k volume in 12 hours. Anyone can manufacture a beautiful wallet history cheaply and then farm the copy-traders who follow it.
- Copying KOL wallets specifically. They are publicly followed, their fills are crowded, and the documented behaviour is that they buy before they post. Their flow is exit liquidity, not entry alpha.
- Fresh wallets. Sophisticated operators rotate wallets, so a wallet with a great 30-day history may have been created 31 days ago specifically to be discovered. Require wallet age materially greater than the scoring window and check the first-funding source.
- Assuming vendor latency marketing is measured. The 10-40ms gRPC, 50-200ms ShredStream advantage, 96% Jito landing rate and 200-300ms landing figures are all vendor-published and unaudited; independent public data shows ordinary inclusion times of 10-60 seconds. Instrument your own slot_delta and realized_slippage_bps on every copy and treat the vendor numbers as an upper bound on your performance.
- No one has published month-over-month persistence for Solana memecoin wallets specifically. Every persistence number in this report is a read-across from Hyperliquid perps or Polymarket. If you do not build the survival measurement yourself, you are flying on analogy.

## Findings

### Correct algorithm for computing realized PnL and win rate on Solana memecoins from raw trade data

Own synthesis, grounded in the vendor semantics above. (1) INGEST: subscribe to Yellowstone gRPC block/transaction updates (or backfill via getSignaturesForAddress + getTransaction with maxSupportedTransactionVersion=0). Decode swaps for pump.fun, PumpSwap, Raydium AMM/CLMM/CPMM/LaunchLab, Meteora DLMM/DBC, Orca Whirlpool, and Jupiter — do NOT rely on Jupiter's outer instruction; parse the inner CPI transfers so a routed swap collapses to one net (wallet, mint, ±token_qty, ∓sol_qty) event per signature. (2) NORMALIZE: SOL, wSOL, USDC, USDT are QUOTE assets and never positions. Convert every leg to both SOL and USD using the block-time price. (3) EXCLUDE NON-TRADES: SPL transfers in/out, mints, airdrops, and burns must not create or consume cost basis — mark the (wallet,mint) as tainted instead. (4) LOTS: maintain a FIFO deque of (qty, cost_in_sol, cost_in_usd, ts) per (wallet, mint). Buy pushes a lot; sell pops FIFO and emits realized_pnl = proceeds - matched_cost. (5) FEES: subtract base fee + priority fee (compute_unit_price × compute_units_consumed / 1e6 lamports) + Jito tip transfers + the platform fee (pump.fun charges a protocol fee on the curve; verify the current rate on-chain rather than hardcoding) + LP fee. Ignoring fees typically overstates a high-frequency wallet's PnL materially. (6) GUARD: if cumulative_sold_qty > cumulative_bought_qty for a pair, mark UNSCORABLE and exclude entirely (this is pnlMode=strict). (7) POSITION CLOSE: a position is CLOSED when remaining qty ≤ dust (use 1e-6 of the position or the token's smallest meaningful unit) or when no activity for 7 days and remaining value < $1. (8) WIN RATE = closed_positions_with_realized_pnl>0 / total_closed_positions — per POSITION, not per transaction; a scalper doing 40 partial sells on one token is one position. (9) UNREALIZED = remaining_qty × current_price - remaining_cost, and must be haircut for illiquidity: mark to the price you'd actually get by simulating a sell of remaining_qty against live reserves, not the last trade price. (10) ROBUST STATS: report median ROI per closed position, payoff ratio, max drawdown on the realized equity curve, and a permutation p-value.

**Actionable:** This is the spec. The three failure modes that will silently break your rankings, in order of damage: routed-swap double counting, unrealized PnL marked at last-trade price on an illiquid bag (inflates every 'top trader' who is bagholding), and sells-without-buys from airdrops/transfers.

`confidence=high` · source: https://docs.solanatracker.io/guides/pnl-v2/overview

### No published study measures month-over-month persistence of Solana memecoin wallet PnL specifically — this gap must be filled with your own measurement

Repeated targeted searching found no dashboard, paper, or report measuring leaderboard overlap between consecutive months for Solana meme wallets, nor a survival curve for copy-traded wallets. The closest proxies are: Hyperliquid ρ=0.52 over adjacent 10-day windows; Polymarket 44% in-sample-skilled remaining skilled out-of-sample (vs ~10% for mutual funds) and ~60% of lucky winners reverting; and repeated practitioner assertions that 'alpha decays and re-running wallet analysis every couple of weeks is necessary' and that 'a wallet exceptionally profitable three months ago is not guaranteed to be sharp today' — none of which is quantified.

**Actionable:** Build the measurement as a first-class part of the system on day one: snapshot your ranked wallet universe weekly, and compute (a) Spearman ρ between consecutive weeks' scores, (b) the fraction of top-decile wallets still top-decile at t+1w/2w/4w/8w, (c) a Kaplan-Meier survival curve for 'wallet remains above your score threshold'. That survival curve IS your re-scoring cadence and your position-sizing decay function. Priors to start with: ρ≈0.4-0.5 at 1-2 weeks, ~44% top-decile survival per month, effectively zero signal beyond 8 weeks without re-verification.
