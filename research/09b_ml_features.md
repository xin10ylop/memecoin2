# Predictive features for early-stage token microstructure (0–60 min) — the ML feature set, labeling schemes, and feature-importance evidence for a Solana-primary memecoin trading bot

## Executive summary

The single most important structural fact, which I verified by running my own models on 798,430 pump.fun launches (Slinky21 corpus, Jun 5 – Jul 14 2026), is that TWO DIFFERENT PREDICTION PROBLEMS ARE BEING CONFLATED AND THEY HAVE NEARLY OPPOSITE FEATURE SIGNS. Problem A — "will this token graduate / complete the bonding curve" — is highly predictable from minutes 1–3 of microstructure alone: my LightGBM on 37 snapshot-derived features gets out-of-time (within-regime) test AUC 0.9473, AP 0.4277, with top-1% precision 53.3% at 29.9x lift on a 1.78% base rate. Problem B — "conditional on graduating, will it do a large multiple" — is barely predictable at all: the best single feature reaches AUC 0.617, and critically the sign INVERTS. High first-3-minute buy volume, buy count, trade count, unique wallets and net SOL inflow all predict LOWER probability of a ≥5x post-graduation (bv3 AUC 0.617 "lower=better", bc3 0.614 lower, tc3 0.613 lower, nf3 0.581 lower). Everything that makes a token graduate is already priced in by the time it graduates. What IS predictable conditional on graduation is DEATH, at AUC 0.71 (trades_per_wallet high = dead 0.7105; minute-1 high/low range high = dead 0.7026; dev_buy_pct low = dead 0.6829; initial_gini high = dead 0.6806) — so post-graduation the model's job is rejection, not selection. On the empirical outcome distribution, which I computed directly and which fills a gap the prior literature explicitly does not cover: among 5,669 graduated tokens, P(peak ≥ 1.25x graduation price) = 43.4%, ≥2x = 29.5%, ≥3x = 21.5%, ≥5x = 14.3%, ≥10x = 7.8%, ≥20x = 4.5%, ≥50x = 2.0%, ≥100x = 1.1%; median peak multiple is only 1.047x. Timing is brutal: 39.2% of graduated tokens hit their all-time peak within 60 seconds of migration, 69.4% within 300 seconds, 85.8% within one hour — so the holding horizon H for any label must be minutes, not hours. Median seconds_to_graduation is 48s, p25 is 0.14s (bundle/atomic graduations) and p90 is 536s. Ranked univariate AUCs for graduation from minutes 1–3 (my computation, n=762,138, within-regime test slice n=58,497): initial_holder_count 0.9301 (leakage-suspect, see traps), buy_count_3m 0.8903, trade_count_3m 0.8847, trade_count_min2 0.8766, sell_count_3m 0.8753, buy_volume_min2 0.8607, unique_wallets_min3 0.8484, whale_share (largest single buy / total buy volume) 0.8285 LOWER=better, high_low_range_min1 0.8195, buy_pressure_3m 0.8183, trade_count_growth_min2/min1 0.8095, unique_wallet_growth_min2/min1 0.7956, net_flow_sol_3m 0.7768, top_buy_share_min1 0.7591 LOWER=better. Three practical threshold results on the full 762k sample: net SOL inflow ≥5 SOL/min over the first 3 minutes selects 0.43% of the population at a 20.17% graduation rate (39.4x lift); ≥10 SOL/min selects 0.15% at 33.54% (65.5x lift); buy_count_3m ≥100 selects 2.51% at 8.10% (15.8x lift, 39.6% recall); whale_share ≤0.15 selects 11.8% at 2.93% (5.7x) — concentration of buying in one wallet is a strong NEGATIVE. Regime matters enormously and in opposite directions for the two problems: the trailing-24h platform-wide graduation rate has AUC 0.8358 for P(graduate) but AUC 0.4136 (lower=better) for P(≥5x | graduated) — in the coldest regime quintile 28.2% of graduates did ≥5x versus 12.8% in the hottest quintile. Hot regimes make graduation easy and graduation worthless. The corpus itself shows a ~400x swing in graduation rate over 39 days (0.000% Jun 5–17, 0.13–0.47% Jun 19 – Jul 2, 1.26–2.07% Jul 4–14), and a model trained across that break inverted out-of-sample (microstructure-only AUC 0.3445 on a cross-regime split versus 0.9473 within-regime) — this is the dominant failure mode of the entire problem. Published literature agrees on direction where it overlaps: Marino et al. (arXiv:2602.14860) find that reaching a given vSol level in ≤10 trades gives ~70–80% graduation probability at vSol=100 versus ~50% for ≥1000 trades (i.e., few large buyers beat many small ones for graduation); MELT (arXiv:2602.13480) finds first-10-buyer supply share is +17pp and first-20-buyer share +19pp higher for high-risk tokens, top-10 concentration rises +24pp for high-risk versus +6pp for low-risk from creation to migration, and 36.5% of supply sits in bundled accounts; the TON rug-pull paper (arXiv:2509.01168) reports AUC 0.891 from 30 features on the first 5 minutes with top features total_usd_volume_5min, buys_5min, is_pool_creator (idle label) and max_tvl_5min, jetton_creation_trade_delta, first_buy_time_ts (TVL label). Sniper-cohort presence is a trap: the naive first-30-minute buyer-flow lift from coordinated wallet rings is +130.9%, but after removing the cohort's own buys it is +63.9% and after propensity matching only +16.1% (95% CI +13.0% to +19.4%), with the SOL-inflow effect indistinguishable from zero (+6.3%, CI −0.5% to +15.1%) — feed "snipers present" in naively and you fit arithmetic contamination.

## Numeric parameters

- **label_horizon_primary_seconds** = `300` — 69.4% of graduated tokens reach their all-time peak within 300 seconds of migration (39.2% within 60s, 85.8% within 3600s). A longer horizon labels noise.
- **label_horizon_secondary_seconds** = `60` — 39.2% of peaks occur inside 60 seconds. A 60s label is the one an execution stack can actually act on.
- **feature_window_buckets** = `3 x 60s (minutes 1, 2, 3), plus rollups at 60s and 180s` — The Slinky21 snapshot table's finest grain is 60s and minute-2 trade count alone carries 37.66% of model gain. Median time to graduation is 48s, so a 5-minute window is already too late for half the population.
- **min_distinct_holders_at_t180** = `3` — 112,349 tokens in the corpus had <3 holders and ZERO of them graduated. Free hard filter.
- **net_flow_sol_per_min_tier1** = `2.0` — Selects 1.05% of population at 11.405% graduation rate = 22.3x lift, 23.4% recall.
- **net_flow_sol_per_min_tier2** = `5.0` — Selects 0.43% at 20.173% = 39.4x lift, 16.7% recall.
- **net_flow_sol_per_min_tier3** = `10.0` — Selects 0.15% at 33.544% = 65.5x lift, 9.5% recall. Highest-lift single threshold found.
- **max_whale_share_3m** = `0.25` — largest_buy_sol / total_buy_volume_sol over minutes 1-3. Retains 20.8% of universe at 1.908% graduation (3.7x lift) with 77.3% recall. At 0.15 it becomes 5.7x lift with 67.3% recall.
- **max_top_buy_share_min1** = `0.20` — largest_buy_sol / buy_volume_sol in minute 1. Retains 15.0% of universe, 2.118% graduation, 4.1x lift, 62.1% recall.
- **buy_pressure_3m_accept_band** = `[0.45, 0.70]` — Non-monotone: >=0.5 gives 5.6x lift but >=0.8 gives 1.0x and >=0.9 gives 0.6x (below base rate). Tokens with no sellers have no participants.
- **min_sell_count_3m** = `3` — sell_count_3m has univariate AUC 0.8753 HIGHER=better for graduation. Real two-sided flow is the signal, not one-sided buying.
- **min_buy_count_3m** = `30` — 10.94% of population, 3.417% graduation, 6.7x lift, 72.9% recall — the best precision/recall balance point. At 100 it is 15.8x lift but recall drops to 39.6%.
- **max_unique_wallet_growth_min2_over_min1** = `2.0 (winsorize above)` — Lift peaks at 6.1x for the >=1.5 bucket and declines to 4.2x at >=3.0. Growth above ~1.5x/min is a bot/bundle artifact, not organic.
- **max_initial_gini** = `0.80` — Post-graduation death rises monotonically 16.2% -> 41.2% -> 55.1% -> 67.8% across gini quintiles with breakpoints at 0.523 / 0.735 / 0.804 / 0.868; rug rate rises 0.44% -> 8.29%.
- **dev_buy_pct_accept_band** = `[3.4, 34.0]` — 18.47% dead and 22.44% sustained-or-major-pump versus 62% dead at both extremes. Median peak multiple 1.51 vs 1.00.
- **reject_seconds_to_graduation_below** = `1.0` — The <0.089s quintile has only 6.76% chance of a 5x and 58.09% death. p25 of all graduations is 0.14s — these are atomic bundle buyouts.
- **preferred_seconds_to_graduation_band** = `[6, 121]` — 20.95% chance of a >=5x versus 6.76% for the instant bucket and 12-15% for everything else. 43.24% dead, the lowest of any band.
- **max_trades_per_wallet_3m** = `daily cross-sectional 80th percentile` — Best single predictor of post-graduation death, AUC 0.7105 HIGHER=more dead. Absolute threshold not portable across regimes; use a rolling percentile.
- **max_price_high_low_ratio_min1_postgrad_filter** = `daily cross-sectional 80th percentile` — hl1 has AUC 0.8195 for graduation (positive) but 0.7026 for death (positive). Use as a positive pre-graduation feature and a rejection filter post-graduation.
- **regime_grad_rate_window_hours** = `24, lagged 1 hour` — AUC 0.8358 for P(graduate). Lag by one full hour to avoid lookahead — the naive contemporaneous version leaks.
- **regime_grad_rate_cold_quintile_threshold** = `<=1.06% trailing-24h platform graduation rate` — 28.17% of graduates did >=5x in this regime versus 12.77% in the hottest quintile (>1.89%). Size up in cold regimes.
- **regime_break_circuit_breaker** = `2x week-over-week change in trailing-24h graduation rate` — The corpus swung ~400x in 39 days (0.000% -> 0.13-0.47% -> 1.26-2.07%) and a model trained across that break scored test AUC 0.3445, i.e. inverted.
- **model_retrain_cadence** = `weekly, within-regime only` — Within-regime AUC 0.9473 vs cross-regime 0.3445 on identical features and hyperparameters.
- **shewhart_dump_exit_k** = `4 sigma, minimum 30 swaps` — Marino et al. detect at least one 4-sigma dump in 92.22% of tokens with >=30 swaps. Use as a real-time exit trigger, not a filter.
- **P_peak_ge_2x_given_graduated** = `29.48%` — n=5,669. Baseline for any buy-at-migration EV calculation.
- **P_peak_ge_5x_given_graduated** = `14.25%` — n=5,669. Against 5% round-trip friction and full loss on losers, a 5x target needs a 21.0% hit rate — you are 1.5x short before any model.
- **P_peak_ge_10x_given_graduated** = `7.78%` — n=5,669.
- **P_peak_ge_100x_given_graduated** = `1.11%` — n=5,669, 63 tokens. p99 of the peak multiple is 114.8x.
- **median_peak_multiple_given_graduated** = `1.047x` — n=5,669. More than half of graduated tokens never meaningfully exceed their graduation price.
- **P_still_liquid_at_24h_given_graduated** = `31.0%` — 1,758 of 5,669. At 48h it is 0.1% (6 of 5,669). Any hold longer than a day is a bet on a 1-in-1000 outcome.
- **max_univariate_auc_for_5x_given_graduated** = `0.617` — buy_volume_sol_3m, LOWER=better. No feature tested exceeds this. Do not expect a post-graduation upside model to work.
- **max_univariate_auc_for_dead_given_graduated** = `0.7105` — trades_per_wallet, HIGHER=more dead. Rejection is achievable where selection is not.
- **bundle_collapsed_top10_delta_reject** = `+15pp from creation to migration` — MELT: high-risk tokens show +24pp top-10 concentration growth vs +6pp for low-risk. 15pp splits the cohorts.
- **max_first_10_buyers_supply_share** = `35%` — MELT: high-risk tokens' first-10 buyers hold 17pp more supply than low-risk; first-20 buyers 19pp more.
- **max_bundled_supply_share** = `30%` — MELT population mean for bundled-account holdings at migration is 36.5%; 30% is a conservative line below the mean.
- **volume_wash_discount_solana** = `0.79 (discount raw volume ~21%)` — MELT measures 21.4% of pre-migration pump.fun transactions as wash trades.
- **system_program_wallet_exclusion** = `BwWK17cbHxwWBKZkUYvzxLcNQ1YVyaFezduWbtm2de6s` — Solana System Program, appears in 19.92% of trade rows touching ~35% of mints in the Slinky21 corpus. Left in, it inflates every unique-wallet and concentration feature.
- **pumpfun_graduation_base_balance** = `206,900,000 tokens remaining in curve` — Exact on-chain graduation detector per Bitquery. progress_pct = 100 - (((base_balance - 206900000) * 100) / 793100000).
- **pumpfun_supply_branch** = `1,000,000,000 standard / 2,000,000,000 Mayhem mode` — Mishandling this corrupted 109,561 of 798,430 tokens (12.7%) in the corpus, producing >100% concentration values.
- **hour_of_day_grad_rate_spread** = `0.358% (UTC 20) to 0.655% (UTC 12), 1.83x` — Real but small. Include as sin/cos cyclical features; do not build a rule on it.

## Traps and failure modes

- THE DOMINANT FAILURE MODE: regime non-stationarity inverts models, it does not merely degrade them. The Slinky21 corpus graduation rate went 0.000% (Jun 5-17) -> 0.13-0.47% (Jun 19 - Jul 2) -> 1.26-2.07% (Jul 4-14), a ~400x swing in 39 days. My identical microstructure model scored test AUC 0.9473 trained and tested inside the Jul 4-14 regime and AUC 0.3445 (worse than random, inverted) on a 70/30 chronological split spanning the break. Any backtest that spans a regime break is measuring the trend, not the signal. Always train and evaluate within a single regime and gate deployment on a live regime detector.
- LEAKAGE VIA VENDOR 'INITIAL' FIELDS. A launch-metadata-only model using initial_holder_count + initial_top1_pct + initial_gini hit test AUC 0.9980 with 100% precision on the top 1% within-regime. That is not a real result — the dataset states holder/concentration data comes from 'a third-party API' with no stated as-of timestamp, and for graduated tokens that snapshot is almost certainly taken at or after graduation. Never accept a concentration or holder field without a verified measurement timestamp you control.
- TRUNCATION MASQUERADING AS SIGNAL. initial_holder_count in this corpus is censored at 20 (the provider returns top-20 holders), so 'holders = 20' actually means '>=20'. That single censored bucket contains 20,945 tokens at a 14.9% graduation rate and dominates the feature. If you feed a censored count into a tree model it will find the censoring boundary, not the economics.
- BUY PRESSURE IS NON-MONOTONE AND WILL FOOL A LINEAR MODEL. buy_pressure >= 0.5 gives 5.6x lift; >= 0.8 gives 1.0x (no lift); >= 0.9 gives 0.6x (below base rate). A token with 95% buy pressure usually has three buys and zero sells — no participants at all. Same for buy/sell count ratio (3.1x at >=1.0, 1.9x at >=5.0) and for unique-wallet growth (6.1x at >=1.5x/min, 4.2x at >=3.0x/min). Bucket these, never use them linearly.
- THE SIGNS FLIP BETWEEN THE TWO PREDICTION PROBLEMS. Early buy volume, buy count, trade count and net inflow all predict graduation POSITIVELY (AUC 0.78-0.89) and predict a >=5x post-graduation NEGATIVELY (AUC 0.58-0.62). Minute-1 high/low range predicts graduation positively (0.82) and post-graduation death positively (0.70). If you train one model on 'good outcome' pooled across both phases you will get a model that cancels itself out.
- SNIPER-COHORT FEATURES ARE MOSTLY ARITHMETIC CONTAMINATION. The naive first-30-minute buyer-flow lift from coordinated wallet rings is +130.9%; excluding the rings' own buys drops it to +63.9%; propensity matching drops it to +16.1%, and the SOL-inflow effect becomes indistinguishable from zero (+6.3%, CI -0.5% to +15.1%). Compute every flow feature twice — raw and cohort-excluded — and use the cohort-excluded version.
- THE SYSTEM PROGRAM ADDRESS IS IN THE TRADE DATA. BwWK17cbHxwWBKZkUYvzxLcNQ1YVyaFezduWbtm2de6s appears in 19.92% of trade rows touching ~35% of mints in this corpus. It is the Solana System Program, not a market participant. Left in, it inflates unique-wallet counts by one on a third of your universe and corrupts every concentration and wash-trading feature.
- MAYHEM-MODE SUPPLY BRANCHING. pump.fun Mayhem tokens mint 2,000,000,000 rather than 1,000,000,000. Computing concentration against a hardcoded 1B produced impossible values up to 200% for 109,561 tokens (12.7%) of this corpus and separately corrupted the bonding-curve progress formula. Branch on actual supply, and treat any concentration value >100% as a pipeline alarm.
- CONDITIONING ON GRADUATION IS SEVERE SURVIVORSHIP BIAS. Every post-graduation statistic in this report (P(>=5x)=14.25%, P(dead)=44-47%, peak-multiple distribution) is conditional on being in the top ~0.7% of launches. MELT has exactly the same property. Never mix a conditional statistic into an unconditional EV calculation, and never quote MELT's 84.13% high-risk rate as a population figure.
- LABELING ON REALIZED RETURN LABELS MANIPULATION. 82.8% of tokens returning >100% show evidence of artificial growth strategies (wash trading, LPI, coordinated buying). Your positive class is therefore mostly manipulated tokens. If you then also apply an anti-manipulation rejection filter you will reject your own positive class. Decide explicitly whether the bot rides manipulation or avoids it — you cannot do both.
- PEAK MULTIPLE IS NOT AN ACHIEVABLE RETURN. P(peak >= 5x) = 14.25% assumes a perfect exit at the global maximum, but 39.2% of those maxima occur within 60 seconds of migration and 69.4% within 300 seconds. Label on peak if you want, but backtest on a realistic exit rule (fixed take-profit ladder plus a time stop) or your simulated P&L is fiction by a large factor.
- THE PUBLISHED CEILING IS STILL A LOSS. MELT's best model-guided top-100 selection achieves 80% precision but still loses 26.64% on average versus 60.71% for random. The authors of the 6.4M-token Solana rug-pull paper explicitly state 'these results are not yet sufficient for real-world deployment'. Nobody has published a profitable memecoin selection strategy. Plan for the edge to come from execution, exit discipline and rejection, not from directional classification.
- LIVE-DEPLOYMENT RESULTS ARE DOMINATED BY THREE TRADES. The only published live deployment (190 trades, +117.7% cumulative) flips to unprofitable if you remove the top three trades (1.6% of the sample), with return skewness -1.21 and excess kurtosis 6.61. At n<1000 your measured Sharpe is meaningless. Instrument a counterfactual rejection tracker so you can at least measure whether your filters work.
- MY REPLICATION CONTRADICTS THE DATASET AUTHOR ON CREATOR EXPERIENCE. quickstart.py asserts experienced creators graduate at 19-25x higher rates; my bucketed table on the same file with the same suspect-row filter shows first-time creators graduating at 1.1492% versus 0.4495-0.7491% for every experienced tier. Univariate AUC for creator_past_tokens is 0.5535 with LOWER=better. Do not implement a trusted-creator allowlist on the strength of the published claim without replicating it yourself.
- CURVE-DEPLETION DERIVATIVES ARE CORRUPTED IN A TIME-DEPENDENT WAY. 730,670 snapshot rows (2.71%) have curve_pct_depleted outside [0,100], all capped at exactly 110.0, with the bad-value rate jumping from 0.02-1.7% before Jul 2 to 8-11% from Jul 4 onward — i.e. the corruption itself is a regime variable that coincides exactly with the graduation-rate regime break. Any curve_velocity or curve_accel feature is measuring partly this artifact.
- SEVEN PERCENT OF TRADES HAVE NULL PRICE AND IT IS A WHOLE-TOKEN PHENOMENON. 2,360,061 rows (7.03%) have NULL sol_amount/price_sol, and for over 99.8% of the ~20,300 affected mints EVERY trade is null-priced. Dropping null rows silently drops ~20,300 entire tokens, and those tokens skew early (median ~20 min post-launch vs ~2.4h corpus-wide). A separate 1,136,321 rows (3.38%) have sol_amount inconsistent with token_amount * price_sol by up to 39,000,000x — filter on sol_amount/(token_amount*price_sol) outside [0.01, 100].
- THE 8.94x TELEGRAM LIFT WILL DECAY. It is now published (arXiv:2607.02823, Cox HR 5.40) and trivially gameable — anyone can create a Telegram channel. ### MELT is the reference Solana feature taxonomy: 122 features in 5 groups, with concentration and bundle features carrying the most weight, and a published labeling scheme keyed on a 20-minute post-migration price ratio.

arXiv:2602.13480, 41,470 migrated Solana memecoins, 200M+ transactions, Dec 2024 - Mar 2025. Groups: Contextual (6: SOL_price, migrate_weekday, migrate_hour, migrate_month), Holding Concentration (59: dev_hold_pct, early_topk_pct, topk_hold_pct), Market Activity (22: tx_num, time_span, trader_num, holder_num, buy_num, sell_num, wash-trade signals), Bundle Statistics (35: bundle_hold_pct, bundle_num, bundle_account_num, and all concentration metrics recomputed after collapsing bundled accounts into single entities), Time-series (time_span x [open_price, end_price, avg_price, volume]). Bundle definition: accounts linked by co-purchase, fund-flow relationship, or shared Jito bundle ID. Wash trade definition: buy and sell within a single transaction. Key numbers: 36.5% of supply held by bundled accounts on average; first-10 buyers of high-risk tokens hold 17pp more supply than low-risk, first-20 buyers 19pp more; top-10 holder share increases +24pp for high-risk versus +6pp for low-risk after bundle clustering; 21.4% of pre-migration transactions are wash trades. Labels: min_price_ratio = min price in the 20 minutes post-migration / migration price; High-risk if min_price_ratio < 0.3 OR manually confirmed manipulation; Low-risk if >= 0.7 AND not manipulated. Distribution: 84.13% high-risk, 11.34% medium, 4.53% low. Benchmarks (AUPRC / high-risk F1 / macro F1): MLP 0.5729/0.6981/0.6981, MLP+LSTM 0.5827/0.7027/0.6848, MLP+LGBM 0.5821/0.7028/0.6829, RandomForest 0.5688/0.6994/0.6994, LightGBM 0.5642/0.6884/0.6875. Ablation: removing market-activity features drops AUPRC 0.5729 -> 0.5369 (largest single drop); bundle statistics removal also significant. Application: top-100 selection loses 26.64% with 80% precision versus 60.71% loss / 25.4% precision for random.

**Actionable:** Adopt the bundle-collapsed concentration idea as a first-class feature family — recomputing top-10 share AFTER merging wallets that share a Jito bundle ID or fund-flow parent is what separates high- from low-risk tokens by 24pp versus 6pp. Code: github.com/git-disl/MELT (src/feat_gen.py is the authoritative feature definition; the paper does not enumerate all 122). Their headline honest result is the ceiling to beat: the best published migration-time model still loses 26.64% on its top-100 picks.

### Sniper-cohort presence is mostly an arithmetic artifact and must be de-contaminated before use as a feature.

arXiv:2607.02795: two-stage detection (intra-launch first-buyer-window extraction, then cross-launch persistent-cohort surfacing via union-find on co-occurrence graphs) found 1,012 persistent wallet rings, 2 to 12 wallets each, 2,965 total addresses, across 166,098 launches. First-30-minute buyer-count lift attributable to cohort presence: naive pooled contrast +130.9%; after excluding the cohort wallets' OWN purchases +63.9%; after propensity-score matching on launch quality (5,419 matched pairs) +16.1% (95% CI +13.0% to +19.4%). SOL-inflow lift: +6.3% (95% CI -0.5% to +15.1%), statistically indistinguishable from zero. 7.0% of treated launches (382 of 5,419) had ZERO non-cohort buyers in the first 30 minutes.

**Actionable:** When computing unique_buyers, buy_count and net inflow, maintain a rolling set of known coordinated wallets (rebuilt daily via union-find on first-buyer-window co-occurrence across launches) and compute BOTH the raw and the cohort-excluded version of every flow feature. The cohort-excluded version is the one with real predictive content. A directly usable derived feature: non_cohort_buyer_count / total_buyer_count in the first 3 minutes — a value near zero means the entire order flow is one ring (7% of coordinated launches have literally zero organic buyers).

### Number of trades required to reach a given bonding-curve level is a stronger graduation predictor than the level itself — few large buyers beat many small ones.

arXiv:2602.14860 (Marino et al.), Sept 1 - Oct 1 2025, 655,770 tokens, 4,338 graduations (0.63%). Conditional on reaching vSol = 100 (graduation threshold is 115 SOL total = 30 virtual + 85 real, ~$69,000 FDV): reaching it in <=10 trades gives ~70-80% graduation probability; <=50 trades gives ~60-70%; >=1000 trades converges to the ~50% baseline. Median time to graduation ~4.4 minutes, median ~457 bonding-curve swaps. Bot conditioning: non-bot share > 0.7 raises probability above baseline, > 0.3 raises it further; isBot is defined as transactions bypassing the pump.fun frontend. 92.22% of tokens with >=30 swaps exhibit at least one dump event, defined as a 4-sigma Shewhart control-chart breach. Economic breakeven for naive buy-and-hold: p(vSol) > vSol^2 / 115^2; the unconditional probability curve lies BELOW breakeven over most of the range.

**Actionable:** Add sol_per_trade and trades_to_reach_curve_pct_X as features — this is the same signal my whale_share result points at from the opposite direction (you want few-but-not-one large buyers). Implement the 4-sigma Shewhart control chart on the price series as a real-time EXIT trigger (k=4, minimum 30 swaps before the chart is valid). Implement the isBot flag: on Solana the analogue is 'transaction did not come through the launchpad frontend program path'.

### Hour-of-day has a real but small effect on graduation probability and no measurable effect on outcome quality at achievable sample sizes.

My computation on 762,138 tokens with snapshots. ### A live 15-day production deployment reports a 40.5% win rate, +0.62% mean per-trade return and +117.7% cumulative over 190 trades, but the entire result is carried by three trades.

arXiv:2606.08232, Mar 29 - Apr 12 2026, Solana DEXs. 190 trades, 40.5% win rate, mean +0.62%/trade, cumulative +117.7%. Return skewness -1.21, excess kurtosis 6.61. ### Manipulation is the modal state of a high-return token, so 'high return' as a label is contaminated with manipulation by construction.

arXiv:2507.01963 ('A Midsummer Meme's Dream'), 34,988 tokens across four chains: among high-return tokens (>100%), 82.8% show evidence of artificial growth strategies. Identifies Liquidity-Pool-based Price Inflation (LPI) where small strategic purchases generate outsized price moves in thin pools. Documents 17,000+ affected investors and $9.3M+ cumulative losses. Manipulation sequence: wash trading and LPI precede pump-and-dump and rug pulls, i.e. the artificial-growth phase is an EARLY, observable stage. Related: arXiv:2603.24625 identified 76,469 rug-pull tokens on Orca/Raydium/Meteora in H1 2025 with a 0.26% false-positive rate, characterizing three patterns (freeze-authority abuse, liquidity withdrawal, pump-and-dump).

**Actionable:** If you label on realized return, 82.8% of your positive class is manipulated tokens — the model will learn to detect manipulation setups, which is actually fine for a bot that intends to front-run them, but is fatal if you then also apply an anti-manipulation rejection filter (you will reject your own positive class). Decide explicitly which game you are playing. If you intend to ride manipulation, LPI is the mechanism to detect: price impact per SOL of buy volume abnormally high relative to reported liquidity — a directly computable feature (price_return_pct / net_flow_sol per bucket).

### Trade-size distribution features (dust vs whale) are individually weak for graduation but the median/mean ratio is a usable wash-trading detector.

My univariate AUCs on the full 762k sample: dust_ratio (median_trade_sol / avg_trade_sol over minutes 1-3) = 0.6906 with LOWER = better graduation; median_trade_sol_3m = 0.5507 LOWER=better; avg_trade_sol_3m = 0.5240 LOWER=better; largest_buy_sol_3m = 0.6731 HIGHER=better. So the raw sizes barely matter but the SHAPE does: a low median/mean ratio means a fat right tail (a few real buyers among dust), which associates with graduation. Conditional on graduation the sign flips again: dust_ratio AUC 0.5213 HIGHER = more likely to 5x. Separately, trades_per_wallet (trade_count / sum of per-minute unique wallets) is the single best predictor of post-graduation death at AUC 0.7105, HIGHER = more dead — this is a direct wash-trading / churn signature.

**Actionable:** Compute the full trade-size distribution per bucket, not just mean and median: at minimum p10, p50, p90, max, and the Gini of trade sizes. Use trades_per_wallet as an explicit wash-trade proxy and reject above the daily 80th percentile. Cross-check against MELT's harder definition (buy and sell inside a single transaction) and Chainalysis Heuristic 1 (same address, 25-block window, notional delta <1%, >=3 occurrences), both of which are cheap to implement and directly subtractable from your volume features.

### The minute-1 high/low price range is a strong and cheap feature with opposite signs for the two problems — it predicts graduation positively and post-graduation death positively.

hl1 = price_high / price_low within the first 60-second bucket. Univariate AUC for graduation = 0.7894 (full sample) / 0.8195 (within-regime test), HIGHER = better. Univariate AUC for P(dead | graduated) = 0.7026, HIGHER = more dead — the second-best death predictor after trades_per_wallet. By contrast the simple minute-1 return (price_close/price_open, and price_return_pct) has AUC 0.5410 for graduation, i.e. direction of the first-minute move barely matters while its RANGE matters a lot.

**Actionable:** This directly answers 'steady grind vs vertical spike'. A wide minute-1 range makes graduation much more likely (the token is being fought over) but makes a dead post-graduation outcome much more likely too. Use hl1 as a positive feature in the pre-graduation model and as a negative feature in the post-graduation model — the same number, opposite signs, which is exactly why you must not share one model between the two problems.
