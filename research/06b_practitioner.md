# What actually-profitable memecoin traders say they do — mechanical, backtestable rules extracted from practitioner content, open-source bot configs, and on-chain datasets (Solana primary; Base/BSC secondary)

## Executive summary

Practitioner content splits cleanly into two economically different games, and conflating them is the single biggest modeling error. Game 1 is bonding-curve scalping (pump.fun pre-graduation): median time-to-graduation is 4.4–9.8 minutes, hold times that actually backtest well are 20–120 SECONDS, take-profit is +30% to +100%, stop-loss is 8–20%, and win rates are 15–20% with the top 5 trades producing ~80% of profit. Game 2 is post-migration swing trading ($60k–$3M MC, hours-to-days holds) where the ladder rules everyone quotes (sell 50% at 2x, 25% at 5x, trail the rest) actually apply. The most credible open-source implementation I found (sergeychernyakov/solana_pulse_bot) ships optimizer-derived LIVE values from a replay sweep over 700 actually-entered paper trades: TP=+30%, hard SL=−8%, max hold=120s, inactivity exit=60s — dramatically tighter than any blog's advice, and its README reports +3.98 SOL over 2,158 closed trades in 13 days at ~18.6% WR. Base rates are brutal and sources broadly agree on magnitude while disagreeing on the digit: graduation rate is 0.63% (arXiv, N=655,770, Sep 2025), 0.84% (N=763,350, Apr 2026), 1.41% and 1.71% (two overlapping Jun–Jul 2026 samples from the same publisher — an internal inconsistency worth noting), and Solidus Labs puts 98.6% of pump.fun tokens as pump-and-dump and 93% of Raydium pools as soft rugs (90%-liquidity-removal threshold). Post-graduation decay is the most actionable dataset I found: of 15,548 graduates, only 56.5% still had ≥$5k liquidity at +5 min, 43.8% at +30 min, 25.8% at +6h, 19.7% at +24h, with median liquidity falling 57% between minute 5 and minute 30 — meaning the "buy the dip after migration" trade is on average buying into a liquidity collapse, not a dip. Holder-concentration thresholds are where sources disagree most: top-10 caps range from 25% (safety checklists) to 30% (Photon conservative preset) to 40% (Axiom late-stage, ML scanner) to 50–80% (permissive bot defaults); the only concentration figure with an attached measured outcome is "top1 ≥80% has ~61% death rate" from the pulse_bot's live data. The highest-lift single entry filter anyone has published with a confidence interval is a dev-reputation filter — dev previously graduated a token AND was inactive ≥7 days → 12.01% graduation rate [95% CI 10.0–14.4%] vs 1.71% baseline, a ~7x lift on N=816 — but the same author's live test of it lost money (77 positions, 6.5% realized graduation, −0.220 SOL) because execution friction pushed the break-even to 8–9%. That negative result is the most important thing in this whole corpus: an edge that survives backtest can die entirely on entry premium and latency, so build fee/slippage/latency modeling into the backtester before trusting any rule below. Two independent arXiv-derived quantities are worth hard-coding: the buy-and-hold breakeven condition p(vSol) > vSol²/115², and the finding that reaching a given SOL level in FEWER swaps (≤10, ≤50) predicts graduation far better than reaching it in 100+ swaps — i.e., liquidity velocity, not liquidity amount. Finally, treat the platform-preset genre (Axiom/Photon/BullX "best settings" pages) with suspicion: most are SEO/AI-generated, they cite each other, and the two genuinely trader-authored presets I found (a Binance Square post and a beehiiv newsletter) are far more specific and far more modest than the guide sites.

## Numeric parameters

- **exit_take_profit_pct (bonding-curve scalp lane)** = `30` — Optimizer-found live value from a replay sweep over 700 actually-entered paper trades (solana_pulse_bot, 2026-04-29). Sweep grid was [50,100,200] and the deployed .env chose 30 — i.e. tighter than the grid, set by the operator after the sweep.
- **exit_hard_stop_loss_pct (bonding-curve scalp lane)** = `8` — Same optimizer run. Code default is 15 (labeled 'tight stop — strict-combo best 2026-04-22'); sweep grid [15,20,30,50]. Deployed value 8.
- **exit_max_hold_seconds (bonding-curve scalp lane)** = `120` — Deployed value; code default 90 with comment 'pump.fun rarely delivers after 90s'. Sweep grid [20, 45, 90, 7200]. Consistent with arXiv median time-to-graduation of 4.4 min and pumpfundata median 9.8 min.
- **exit_inactivity_seconds** = `60` — Deployed value; no trades for 60s = token dead, exit. Sweep grid [30,60,120,300].
- **exit_trailing_stop_activation_pct / distance_pct** = `50 / 50 (code default); sweep [30,50,80] / [15,30,50]` — pulse_bot defaults; 15% is described as 'tight, tuned for pump.fun's fast reversals', 50% as lenient.
- **exit_peak_buy_rate_drop_ratio** = `0.3` — Momentum-fade exit: sell when buy_rate falls to 0.3x peak_buy_rate, with a floor requiring peak_buy_rate >= 0.3 before the rule can fire. Chosen as mid-range of a 0.2/0.3/0.4 sweep.
- **exit_sell_pressure_ratio** = `1.0` — Exit when sell_rate exceeds buy_rate by this factor. Sweep grid [0.7, 1.0, 1.5].
- **Multi-tier TP ladder (post-migration swing lane)** = `TP1: sell 30% at +50% and move stop to breakeven; TP2: sell 50% at +200%; trail final 20% at 15% below high` — Outperformed single TP+100%/SL-40% in a 200-trade replay (Jan 15 - May 15 2026, MC $20k-$400k, >=8 SOL liq, 1.5% slippage, 0.25 SOL/exit): expectancy +0.82R vs +0.41R, max DD -10.1R vs -18.3R, profit factor 2.34 vs 1.52.
- **Retail consensus ladder (alternative to backtest)** = `sell 50% at 2x, 30% at 5x, trail 20% from 10x` — Most frequently repeated ladder across independent guides (solanatools, madeonsol aggressive, memecointradingterminals, walletbot).
- **Dynamic tightening trail** = `entry→2x: 40%; 2x→5x: 30%; 5x→10x: 20%; >10x: 15%` — madeonsol; the only fully-specified regime-dependent trail found. Pairs with time-decay: tighten 10% after 4h without a new high, another 10% after 12h, full exit at 24h.
- **top10_holder_pct max** = `25 (strict) / 40 (moderate) / 100 (disabled) — SWEEP, do not fix` — Sources range 25%-80% with no consensus. 25% from safety checklists, 40% from ML-scanner defaults and Axiom late-stage presets, 60-80% from permissive bot defaults.
- **top1_holder_pct max** = `80` — The only concentration threshold with an attached measured outcome: pulse_bot live data shows top1 >= 80% has ~61% death rate. Sweep [40, 60, 80, 100].
- **top1_delta_pct (T+30s to T+120s)** = `max +5, min -10` — Positive delta = dev accumulating (rug signal); negative = distributing (organic). Sweep max over [0, 5, 10, 100] and min over [-100, -10, -5, 0].
- **bundled_supply_pct** = `<5 pass, 5-15 warn, 15-30 heavy penalty, >30 reject` — Bubblemaps official bands, corroborated exactly by DeFade checklist; Solana Tracker independently flags >20% as 'serious red flag'.
- **largest_connected_cluster_pct** = `<15 healthy, 15-30 elevated, >30 reject, >50 extreme` — Bubblemaps official bands.
- **dev_holding_pct max** = `5 (strict) / 10 (moderate)` — Median of a tight cluster: >5% 'serious red flag' (solanatools, coincodecap), max 6% (practitioner post), <5%/<3% (photonguides), 10% (Axiom presets). Note >=1 source warns dev holding of exactly 0% 'can mean abandonment'.
- **min_unique_buyers (graduation-targeting)** = `20` — pumpfundata: 'Any graduated token with fewer than 20 buyers was almost certainly a bot'; real graduates median 312 unique buyers vs 6 for bot launches.
- **buy_volume / sell_volume ratio min** = `1.5` — pumpfundata: median buyer-to-seller volume ratio of real graduates = 1.57x, and 100% of real graduates had buy volume > sell volume.
- **buy_txn_share min (flow filter)** = `0.70 sustained 30+ minutes` — chaindecrypt DexScreener guide; low-confidence source, sweep [0.55, 0.60, 0.70].
- **vol24h / market_cap ratio min** = `1.0` — Widely cited 'healthy momentum' threshold (24h volume >= market cap). Low-confidence single source; sweep [0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0].
- **curve_progress_pct = real_sol_in_curve / 85** = `graduation at 85 SOL real reserves (115 SOL incl. 30 virtual), ~$69k MC` — Confirmed by arXiv 2602.14860 and pump.fun-derived guides. Track SOL, not USD — the USD figure drifts with SOL price.
- **Buy-and-hold-to-graduation breakeven** = `p(vSol) > vSol^2 / 115^2` — Derived in arXiv 2602.14860. At 40 SOL requires p>12.1%, at 60 SOL p>27.2%, at 85 SOL p>54.6% — mathematically rules out the naive late-curve entry.
- **Dev-reputation entry gate** = `prior_graduations >= 1 AND days_since_last_launch >= 7` — 12.01% graduation [95% CI 10.0-14.4%] on N=816 vs 1.71% baseline (~7x). BUT live test realized only 6.5% against an 8-9% break-even and lost money.
- **Break-even hit rate (graduation strategy with real execution)** = `8-9%` — Empirically derived by the same author whose backtested 12% signal failed live. Use as the minimum bar any graduation-targeting rule must clear by a safety margin.
- **min_liquidity_usd** = `2,500 (curve entry) / 15,000-30,000 (post-migration) / 50,000 (mid-stage momentum)` — Anon-tradebot uses 2,500 for sub-10s curve entries; solana-ml-scanner 15,000; altrady 'below $30,000 your trade moves price against you'; altrady mid-stage preset 50,000.
- **liquidity floor relative to position** = `position_size <= liquidity / 10 (photonguides); hard cap position <= 1% of pool, never >5%` — Two independent formulations of the same constraint. Scorp Labs: <=1% negligible impact, 2-3% acceptable with scaled exits, >5% 'you become the market'.
- **risk per trade** = `1-2% of bankroll in RISK (not notional); position = (bankroll x risk%) / stop_distance%` — Scorp Labs; corroborated by solanatools (<=1% of stack), altrady (0.5-1%), walletbot (1-2%).
- **fractional Kelly** = `f*/4 to f*/10` — Scorp Labs: retains ~75% of growth while cutting variance ~4x vs full Kelly.
- **drawdown-from-peak sizing ladder** = `0 to -15%: 2% risk; -15% to -25%: 1% risk; >-25%: full stop, 24-48h reset` — Scorp Labs. The only published mechanical de-risking ladder found.
- **max_concurrent_positions** = `3-5` — solana-ml-scanner 3, solanatools 3, okx plugin-store 5, memegateway 5-8. pulse_bot runs 30 for a high-frequency scalping portfolio — scale with strategy frequency.
- **daily / session kill switch** = `daily_loss_limit -20% (ML scanner); stop for the day at -5 to -10% (walletbot); pause 600s after 3 consecutive losses (okx)` — Three independent implementations of the same safety mechanism. Implement all three layers: per-day %, consecutive-loss pause, absolute session SOL stop.
- **CONSECUTIVE_FILTER_MATCHES** = `3 (polling every 2s over a 60s window)` — fdundjer/solana-sniper-bot default: a filter must pass 3 consecutive checks before firing. Cheap and effective anti-noise / anti-flash-manipulation mechanism.
- **sell slippage multiplier vs buy** = `1.5x` — pulse_bot execution model: execution_base_slippage=0.02, execution_slippage_per_volume_pct=0.05, execution_max_slippage=0.25, execution_sell_slippage_mult=1.5. Exits are systematically more expensive than entries — most naive backtests miss this.
- **anti-chase gates** = `reject if within 4% of local high AND +90% from first print; require >=8% pullback above MC threshold; reject if +30-35% in last ~1.5-3s; reject if +60% from first observed price in the fast lane` — Anon-tradebot production late_entry_* parameters. Directly encodes 'do not buy the vertical green candle'.
- **entry market cap band (bonding-curve lane)** = `$7,000 floor / $35,000 ceiling; soft late-entry threshold $18,000, hard $28,000` — Anon-tradebot RuleParams defaults. Corroborated loosely by practitioner presets: $5.5k-$8.5k new, <=$45k pre-graduation.
- **entry market cap band (post-migration lane)** = `$60,000-$100,000 (practitioner preset) or $20,000-$400,000 (backtest) or $70,000-$3,000,000 (screener guide)` — Three different bands from three source types — sweep the whole $60k-$3M range and let data pick. The 200-trade backtest with positive expectancy used $20k-$400k.
- **post-migration liquidity survival gate** = `require liquidity_now >= liquidity_at_t+5min; expect 43.8% survival at +30min, 19.7% at +24h` — N=15,548 graduates. Median liquidity falls 57% between minute 5 and minute 30 — a price 'dip' in this window is usually a liquidity collapse, not an entry.
- **copy-trade break-even** = `~14% at 0.1 SOL, ~4.4% at 0.5 SOL, ~3.2% at 1 SOL, ~2.6% at 2 SOL` — GMGN 1% each way + ~0.012 SOL fixed on-chain cost. Sets a hard minimum viable position size.
- **smart-money wallet gate** = `>=30 closed trades in 90d, expectancy >= +0.05 SOL/trade, PnL HHI < 0.15, alpha > 0, must survive a 30-day out-of-sample holdout` — Scorp Labs 5-filter framework. Note: 62% of high-ROI wallets had negative alpha in their Apr-May 2026 sample.
- **rug label definition (for supervised training)** = `>=90% of liquidity removed` — Solidus Labs' published detection threshold, used to produce the 93%-of-Raydium-pools figure. The only rug definition with an explicit numeric cutoff.
- **pump.fun fee model** = `1% each side pre-graduation; 0.25% each side on PumpSwap post-graduation (0.05% to creator); migration constant 15,000,001 lamports` — Fees differ 4x across the graduation boundary — must be modeled as two regimes.

## Traps and failure modes

- THE BIG ONE: a backtested edge can die entirely on execution friction. The best-documented case — a dev-reputation filter with 12.01% graduation [CI 10.0-14.4%] vs 1.71% baseline — realized only 6.5% over 77 live positions and lost 0.220 SOL, because break-even with real entry premium and latency was 8-9%. Build fee/slippage/latency/fill-probability modeling into the backtester on day one, and require any rule to clear break-even by >=1.5x.
- Optimizing on AUC/precision instead of PnL. pulse_bot's entry model hit AUC 0.96 and 11x lift over base rate while its economic backtest was NEGATIVE (-1.96 SOL). Rank models on realized PnL after fees, never on classification metrics.
- Label truncation bias. pulse_bot documents that models trained on labels truncated at 90 seconds make every selective strategy -EV, because 'winners don't have time to grow to +50/+100%'. Your label horizon silently defines your strategy — set it deliberately and test multiple horizons.
- Momentum-bias leakage. pulse_bot's KNOWN_LEAK_FEATURES list: market_cap_sol, mc_at_scoring, sol_to_graduation, v_sol_in_bonding_curve, v_tokens_in_bonding_curve, log_market_cap, token_price_sol. These dominated feature importance and killed the economic backtest. Any feature that is a monotone function of 'how far it has already pumped' will leak.
- Conflating the two games. Pre-graduation scalping (hold 20-120s, TP +30%) and post-migration swinging (hold hours-days, TP ladder to 10x) have opposite optimal parameters. A single parameter set across both will be mediocre at both.
- Buying the 'post-migration dip' blind. Median liquidity falls 57% between +5min and +30min after migration; only 43.8% of graduates still have $5k liquidity at +30min and 19.7% at +24h. Most 'dips' in that window are exit liquidity draining, not accumulation. Gate on liquidity trajectory, not price.
- Chasing near-graduation tokens. The arXiv breakeven p(vSol) > vSol^2/115^2 means at 85 SOL in curve you need >54.6% graduation probability just to break even on a buy-and-hold-to-graduation trade. The 'final stretch' tab is mathematically the worst place to buy that trade.
- Counter-intuitive momentum inversions found in real data: high positive curve acceleration correlates with hard stops (mean +0.25 for stopped trades vs -0.04 for wins), and HIGH early momentum score correlates with rug (stop mean 25.5 vs win mean 19.9). Naive 'buy the strongest momentum' logic is actively harmful at this timescale.
- Fake holder counts. ATA rent is 0.00203928 SOL, so 1,000 fake holders cost ~2.04 SOL (~$408), and compressed accounts make it ~400x cheaper (~0.005 SOL per 1,000). One worked example showed 92% of a headline holder count evaporating after dust filtering. Never use raw holder_count — filter by value-denominated dust floor and cross-check against unique_buyers.
- Bot-manufactured graduations. 23.7% of April 2026 graduations were bot-driven, including 735 tokens bonded in a single block with zero community participation. If you train on 'graduated = success' you will learn to predict bots. Exclude graduates with <20 unique buyers from training labels.
- Wash trading inflates every volume-based filter. No source publishes numeric wash-detection cutoffs, so you must derive your own: txns/unique-makers ratio, trade-size uniformity, matched round trips within seconds, and buy/sell count symmetry per window.
- Survivorship and one-lucky-trade bias in wallet selection. 62% of high-ROI wallets showed negative market-neutral alpha in one Apr-May 2026 sample; a wallet with +38.4 SOL total where one trade contributed +30 SOL has a PnL HHI of 0.71. Always require >=30 trades, HHI<0.15, and an out-of-sample holdout.
- Sell-side slippage asymmetry. Exits cost ~1.5x entries in pulse_bot's execution model, and post-migration pools halve in depth within 30 minutes. A backtest that uses symmetric slippage will show profitable exits you cannot actually take.
- Fixed-cost floor makes micro-sizing structurally unprofitable: ~14% break-even at 0.1 SOL vs ~3.2% at 1 SOL on GMGN-style routing. Choosing small size 'to be safe' can make an otherwise-positive edge negative.
- Degenerate parameter sweeps. pulse_bot explicitly guards against combos where min>=max (which starve the sweep of trades) and requires >=20 trades before a combo can enter the leaderboard, because 0-trade combos have pnl=0 which 'beats' losing combos. Any naive grid search will otherwise select do-nothing configurations.
- The 'best settings' guide genre is largely SEO/AI-generated and self-citing (axiompedia, memecoinnavigator, photonguides, madeonsol, moonhydra, gmgnguide, solanatools, chaindecrypt all share style and cross-reference each other). Their numbers are plausible seeds but carry no evidence. Named-trader 'rules' articles (e.g. 'Cupsey's rules') were unretrievable — the Medium account is suspended (HTTP 410).
- Marketing claims to discard entirely: 'turn $500 into 6 figures', '20-30 SOL ($5k) a day', '1 SOL to 1000 SOL', and the widely-repeated 'even pros have 50-60% win rates' (measured figures are 18.6% for a scalping bot and 38-41% for a mid-stage swing backtest).
- Graduation-rate sources disagree: 0.63% / 0.84% / 1.41% / 1.71% / '<2%'. Two of these (1.41% and 1.71%) come from the same publisher over overlapping June-July 2026 windows, which undercuts both. Use ~1% as a planning figure and treat anything you measure above ~3% as a leakage alarm.
- Venue risk: one source claims 'BullX hasn't accepted a trade since 1 June 2026' — unverified, but a reminder that terminal APIs disappear. Prefer direct on-chain routing (Jupiter/PumpSwap) over terminal-bot APIs for anything load-bearing.
- Base and BSC have no equivalent public dataset. Clanker launches straight to a Uniswap pool with no bonding curve, so the entire graduation strategy class does not exist there. Do not port Solana thresholds — build your own baselines first.
- Reddit, YouTube, X and Medium were all inaccessible from this environment (403/IP-blocked/suspended). The practitioner-voice sampling in this report is therefore biased toward indexable web content and open-source code, and under-samples the trench-Twitter/Discord channels where the most current tactics actually circulate. Treat that as a known coverage gap, not as evidence those sources say the same thing.

## Findings

### The best-documented open-source pump.fun bot ships optimizer-derived LIVE exit values that are radically tighter than blog advice: TP +30%, hard SL −8%, max hold 120s, inactivity exit 60s

From sergeychernyakov/solana_pulse_bot .env.example, commented 'Optimizer-found values from replay sweep on 700 actually-entered paper_trades (2026-04-29). Best combo by total simulated PnL.': PULSE_EXIT_TAKE_PROFIT_PCT=30, PULSE_EXIT_HARD_STOP_LOSS_PCT=8, PULSE_EXIT_MAX_HOLD_SECONDS=120, PULSE_EXIT_INACTIVITY_SECONDS=60. The code-level defaults in pulse_bot/config.py are looser (exit_take_profit_pct=100.0, exit_hard_stop_loss_pct=15.0, exit_max_hold_seconds=90.0, exit_inactivity_seconds=120.0, exit_trailing_stop_activation_pct=50.0, exit_trailing_stop_distance_pct=50.0), plus partial-exit params exit_partial_on_profit_pct=0.30 at exit_profit_threshold_pct=200.0, exit_partial_on_weak_pulse_pct=0.50 at exit_weak_pulse_min_profit_pct=50.0, exit_moonbag_pct=0.10.

**Actionable:** Implement two exit profiles keyed to venue phase. Pre-graduation scalp profile: TP=+30%, SL=−8%, hard timeout=120s, inactivity(no trades)=60s. Post-graduation swing profile: TP ladder + trailing. Backtest both; do not use one set of exit constants across both phases.

### That same bot's published performance defines a realistic profitability envelope: ~18.6% win rate, top-5 winners ≈80% of profit, +0.0018 SOL average per trade

README table: 'v21 ml_override era (2026-04-30 → 2026-05-13, 13 days) | 2158 closed | +3.98 SOL | +0.0018 SOL/trade' ≈ +0.28 SOL/day. ### Copy-trading round-trip fee math makes small positions structurally unprofitable — break-even is ~14% at 0.1 SOL and ~3.2% at 1 SOL

gmgnguide fee table: 0.1 SOL buy → GMGN 1% both ways 0.002 SOL + on-chain 0.012 SOL = 0.014 SOL round-trip = ~14% break-even. 0.5 SOL → 0.022 total = ~4.4%. 1 SOL → 0.032 = ~3.2%. 2 SOL → 0.052 = ~2.6%. Priority fee guidance 0.006 SOL+ for auto buy/sell/limit; 0.002-0.006 when speed non-critical. Max 10 concurrent copy tasks; 20-token blacklist; copy task pauses after 3 consecutive failures. solanatools independently: 'At 0.9% per trade and 20 trades/week, you need 1.8% weekly return just to break even.' Structural lag: 'you buy higher than the target on the way up... the same lag puts you behind it on the way out, into a book that the target's own sell just made thinner.'

**Actionable:** Compute break_even_pct = (2*platform_fee_pct) + (fixed_onchain_cost_sol / position_sol) for every prospective trade and REJECT trades whose expected move does not clear it by a margin. This alone eliminates most micro-sizing strategies. Set a minimum position size derived from your fixed per-trade cost, not from risk appetite.

### Rigorous wallet-vetting criteria for copy-trading / smart-money signals, with a statistical framework rather than vibes

Scorp Labs 5 filters: (1) ≥30 closed trades in 90 days (at 30 trades and 60% WR the 95% CI is ±18pp); (2) expectancy ≥ +0.05 SOL/trade — example rejection: 80% winners at +5% and 20% losers at −80% = −0.12 SOL expectancy; (3) PnL concentration HHI <0.15 healthy, 0.15–0.30 caution, >0.30 reject (example: one +30 SOL trade out of +38.4 total = HHI 0.71, rejected); (4) market-neutral alpha >0 vs a 50-token liquid memecoin benchmark — 'April–May 2026 data showed 62% of high-ROI wallets had negative alpha'; (5) out-of-sample holdout — train on first 60 days, require filters 1-4 to independently pass on a never-scored 30-day holdout. Cruder versions elsewhere: memegateway '60%+ win rate over 50+ trades is solid, under 40% unreliable... 200+ trades and 65% WR is a pattern you can trust'; solanatools '90-day PnL above 300% and at least 50 trades'; GMGN flags wallets with 'buy-sell pairs executed inside ten seconds' as bots.

**Actionable:** Implement wallet scoring exactly as: min_closed_trades=30 (90d), expectancy_sol>=0.05, HHI(pnl)<0.15, alpha>0 vs benchmark basket, plus a 60/30-day train/holdout split. The HHI filter is the highest-value piece — it is the only published mechanism that separates 'one lucky 100x' from repeatable skill, and it is trivially computable as sum((pnl_i/total_pnl)^2) over winning trades.

### Position sizing: 1-2% risk per trade, fractional Kelly at 1/4 to 1/10, and a hard liquidity cap of ≤1-3% of pool

Scorp Labs: risk 1-2% of bankroll (risk, not position size); Position size = (Bankroll × Risk%) / Stop-loss distance%. Fractional Kelly 1/4 to 1/10 of f* — 'retains roughly 75% of growth while reducing variance by ~4x'. **Actionable:** Implement position sizing as min(risk_based, liquidity_based) where liquidity_based = 0.01 * pool_liquidity_usd, and layer the drawdown ladder as a global multiplier on risk% driven by equity-from-peak.
