# Rigorous quantitative literature and data studies on memecoin returns (Solana/pump.fun primary; Base/BSC secondary) — base rates, survival, rug/wash fractions, trader profitability, and strategy backtests

## Executive summary

The published literature converges on a brutal set of base rates. Graduation (bonding curve completion, ~$69k mcap) has run between 0.2% and 1.8% of launches through most of 2024-2026, spiking to 4.7-6.7% only after pump.fun's BOOST mechanic launched in late July 2026 (vs ~0.84% in June 2026) — so graduation rate is a REGIME variable, not a constant, and any bot economics must be re-estimated monthly. Because pump.fun launch FDV is ~$4-5k and graduation FDV is ~$69k, the graduation rate is approximately P(~15x from block-0 price); that means the true unconditional base rate of a 15x is roughly 1%, and of a 5x-from-launch perhaps single-digit percent. No peer-reviewed paper publishes a clean 2x/5x/10x/50x/100x peak-multiple histogram — this is the single biggest gap in the literature, and I could not verify any such distribution; do not trust any number you see quoted for it. The best proxy is the MemeTrans/MELT dataset (41,470 fully-migrated Solana memecoins, Dec 2024–Mar 2025): within 20 minutes of migration, 60.26% of tokens trade below 0.2x of migration price, 73% below 0.4x, and only 5.20% never trade below migration price at all. That is the decisive number for any "buy at graduation" strategy. Survival is equally harsh: CoinGecko's 18.67M-token study (Jan 2024–Jun 2026) finds 68.67% record their last trade on launch day, 80.37% within one day, and only 4.55% are still trading after 90 days. Manipulation is the default state, not the exception: Solidus Labs flags 98.6% of ~7M pump.fun tokens as rug/pump-and-dump (only ~97k ever held >$1,000 liquidity) and 93% of 388,000 Raydium pools as soft rugs (median rug size $2,832); Marino et al. find 92.22% of tokens with ≥30 swaps show at least one 4-sigma dump event; MELT labels 84.13% of migrated tokens high-risk. Wash trading estimates disagree violently by chain and method — MELT measures 21.4% of pre-migration pump.fun transactions as wash trades, while Chainalysis's conservative heuristics find only 0.035-0.046% of ETH/BNB/Base DEX volume — treat the Solana figure as the operative one. Retail profitability sources also disagree sharply: CoinGecko (3.14M wallets, realized PnL, Apr 2026) reports 73.28% profitable, but its own methodology note admits it counts realized PnL only, excluding never-sold bags, which mechanically excludes the modal total loss; a Dune-based 90-day Solana DEX meme study (Aug 2026, 304,161 wallets) reports 6.25% profitable with median loss $120 and only 25 wallets above $10,000. The crypto.news 29.6M-wallet study (Aug 2024) is the best-calibrated middle ground: 37.6% positive PnL but only 0.76% above $1,000 and 0.046% above $10,000 — profits are extremely power-law concentrated. Galaxy's Oct 2025 report finds 12 of ~12.9M pump.fun tokens hold >55% of the $4.8B aggregate FDMC and that median Solana memecoin hold time collapsed to 100 seconds (from 300 a year earlier). Critically, the only published backtest-like result — MELT's model-guided top-100 selection — achieves 76% precision but still loses 26.64% on average, versus 60.71% for random selection. In other words, the best published ML on migration-time features converts a catastrophic loss into a merely bad one; nobody has published a profitable memecoin selection strategy. Fee math: pump.fun bonding curve costs 1.25% per side (0.95% protocol + 0.30% creator), PumpSwap scales 1.25% down to 0.30% by market cap, plus priority fees, Jito tips, and sandwich losses (~3% total cost on an unprotected 1 SOL Raydium memecoin swap vs ~0.7% protected). Budget 4-6% round-trip friction. At that friction, a 5x-target strategy with full loss on losers needs a 21.0% hit rate; a 10x target needs 10.5%; a 2x target needs 52.5%. Against a ~5.2% base rate of a migrated token not even dipping below entry, the naive strategies are not close, and the entire edge must come from selection (bundle/concentration/social features) and from exit discipline, not from directional exposure.

## Numeric parameters

- **min_pool_liquidity_usd** = `1000` — Solidus Labs' own cutoff: fewer than ~100k of ~7M pump.fun tokens ever held >$1,000 liquidity. Below this you are definitionally inside the 98.6% rug/manipulation population.
- **max_position_pct_of_quote_reserves** = `1.0%` — Median Raydium soft rug is $2,832 and 25% are under $732 (Solidus). If your position is a meaningful fraction of pool depth you are the exit liquidity and cannot exit.
- **max_bundled_supply_share** = `30%` — MELT measures bundled accounts holding 36.5% of supply as the population mean for coordinated launches; 30% is a conservative reject line below that mean.
- **max_first_10_buyers_supply_share** = `35%` — MELT: high-risk tokens show first-10-buyer share +17pp and first-20-buyer share +19pp above the low-risk cohort.
- **max_top10_holder_concentration_growth** = `+15pp from creation to migration` — MELT: high-risk tokens show +24pp top-10 concentration increase vs +6pp for low-risk. 15pp splits the two cohorts.
- **shewhart_dump_detector_k** = `4 sigma` — Marino et al. use k=4 on a Shewhart control chart and detect at least one dump in 92.22% of tokens with >=30 swaps. Use as a real-time exit trigger.
- **min_swaps_before_scoring** = `30` — Marino et al. restrict dump analysis to tokens with >=30 swaps; below that the price series is too sparse for the control chart.
- **wash_trade_heuristic_window** = `25 blocks (~5 min), notional delta <1%, >=3 occurrences per address` — Chainalysis Heuristic 1 verbatim. Cheap to implement, directly subtractable from volume features.
- **wash_trade_score_threshold_c** = `50` — arXiv:2601.08641 threshold on the ratio of matched buy-sell pairs to net position for flagging a wash-trading wallet.
- **volume_discount_factor_solana** = `0.80 (discount raw volume by ~20%)` — MELT: 21.4% of pre-migration pump.fun transactions are wash trades.
- **manipulation_classifier_reject_threshold** = `>=0.70` — MemeTrans high-risk rule: min_price_ratio < 0.3 OR manipulation score >= 0.7 (TCN at 87.85% accuracy / 0.9320 AUC).
- **min_price_ratio_label_window** = `20 minutes post-migration` — MemeTrans selected y=20 min as balancing insider sell-off completion against longer-term market decay. Reproducible label definition.
- **round_trip_friction_c** = `5.0% (range 2.5%-6%)` — 1.25% per side on the pump.fun curve (0.95% protocol + 0.30% creator), PumpSwap 1.25% scaling to 0.30% by mcap, plus ~2% net slippage/MEV/priority fees.
- **max_slippage_tolerance** = `3-5%` — Sandwich bots specifically target memecoin traders' wide slippage settings; unprotected 1 SOL swaps lose ~3% vs ~0.7% protected (Helius / practitioner data).
- **pumpswap_fee_floor_mcap** = `98,240 SOL market cap` — Point at which PumpSwap canonical-pool total fee reaches its 0.30% floor from 1.25% at 0-420 SOL.
- **max_hold_seconds_default** = `300` — Galaxy: median Solana memecoin hold time is 100 seconds (was 300 a year prior); median time-to-graduation is ~4.4 min. Anything longer is a deliberate deviation from the market.
- **max_hold_hours_hard_kill** = `24` — CoinGecko: 80.37% of tokens record their last trade within one day of launch. A position older than 24h in a non-graduated token is likely unexitable.
- **grad_rate_regime_bands** = `defensive <0.5%, normal 0.5-3%, aggressive >3%` — Observed range: 0.198% (May-Jun 2026), 0.63% (Sep-Oct 2025), 0.84% (Jun 2026), 1.15% (Feb 2026), 1.79% (Aug 2024), 4.7-6.7% (post-BOOST Jul 2026).
- **breakeven_hit_rate_5x** = `21.0% at L=1.0, c=5%; 8.1% at L=0.30 with stops` — p = (L+c)/(M-1+L). Shows stop-loss discipline moves break-even ~2.6x more than any published model improvement.
- **breakeven_hit_rate_2x** = `52.5% at L=1.0, c=5%; 26.9% at L=0.30` — Same formula, M=2. Compare to the 5.20% of migrated tokens that never dip below migration price.
- **breakeven_hit_rate_10x** = `10.5% at L=1.0, c=5%; 8.7% at L=0.80` — Same formula, M=10. Population rate of a ~15x from launch is 0.2-1.8%, so a 10x strategy needs >10x model lift.
- **stop_loss_pct** = `-25% to -35%` — 60.26% of migrated tokens fall below 0.2x within 20 min; a tight stop is what converts L from ~0.85 to ~0.30 and is the largest single lever on break-even.
- **required_social_channels** = `>=1 (Telegram) preferred >=3, website must return HTTP 200` — Telegram gives 8.94x graduation lift (Cox HR 5.40); all three channels 17.4x. MemeChain: 67.91% of claimed websites are dead, so liveness check separates the cohort further.
- **pumpfun_graduation_threshold** = `85 real SOL (115 total incl. 30 virtual), ~$69,000 FDV` — Marino et al.; defines the bonding-curve completion point and the ~15x-from-launch multiple.
- **min_landed_tx_rate_alarm** = `90%` — arXiv:2607.28424: viable bot clusters run 91.8-99.5% success; failing clusters sit under 10.5%. Below 90% you are burning fees.
- **creator_blacklist_rule** = `any prior rugged token = hard reject; trust creator only with >=10 prior launches and >5% historical graduation ratio` — Chainalysis: 94% of pump-and-dump pools rugged by the deployer. Marino: top creator with 107 launches had an 8.4% graduation ratio vs ~0.63% population.
- **scheme_age_gate** = `7 days` — Chainalysis: average duration before deployer abandonment is 6.23 days; only 1% of schemes last 4-5 months.

## Traps and failure modes

- NO published peak-multiple distribution exists. I could not find a single credible source giving the % of memecoins that ever do 2x/5x/10x/50x/100x from launch. Anything you see quoting such a table is almost certainly fabricated or extrapolated. You must generate this yourself from an indexed archive — it is the highest-value missing input to your economics, and it is a one-week data job.
- Survivorship bias in every public dataset. MemeChain reports 0.09% one-day mortality for Solana while CoinGecko reports 68.67% on the full population — a 700x discrepancy driven entirely by sample selection. MELT/MemeTrans only contains tokens that MIGRATED (the top ~1%), so its statistics are conditional on graduation and say nothing about the other 99%. Never mix conditional and unconditional base rates in the same EV calculation.
- Realized-PnL-only accounting flatters profitability enormously. CoinGecko's 73.28%-profitable April 2026 figure explicitly excludes wallets that bought and never sold — i.e. the modal memecoin outcome. This is why the same period's alternative studies show 6.25%. Your own backtest must mark unsold positions to zero, not exclude them.
- The graduation rate is not stationary and mechanics changes invalidate models overnight. It went from ~0.84% in June 2026 to 6.7% in late July 2026 purely because pump.fun shipped BOOST (redeploying ~20% of migration liquidity into post-migration market buys followed by burns). Every model fit before a mechanics change is stale. Subscribe to launchpad changelogs as a first-class data feed.
- arXiv:2607.02823's headline 0.198% graduation rate is NOT comparable to Marino's 0.63%. The author discloses the collector's effective coverage window was ~6 minutes, not the claimed 24 hours, making all its absolute rates lower bounds. Its relative effects (social-channel lifts, Cox HRs) are more trustworthy than its levels. The paper's structure (versioned corrigenda, single author, rapid turnaround) also warrants independent replication before you rely on it.
- Sniper-cohort presence is a weak signal that looks strong. The naive uncontrolled association between coordinated wallets and first-hour buyer flow is +130.9%; after excluding the cohort's own buys it drops to +63.9%; after propensity matching on launch quality it is +16.1% on buyer count and statistically indistinguishable from zero (+6.3%, CI −0.5% to +15.1%) on SOL inflow. If you naively feed 'snipers present' into a model you will fit contamination, not signal.
- The best-published selection model still loses 26.64%. Do not plan a business around a directional edge from a classifier. The MELT top-100 result (76% precision, −26.64%) versus random (25.4%, −60.71%) is the honest ceiling of migration-time feature engineering as of the current literature.
- Wide slippage tolerance is how memecoin traders get sandwiched. Both Chainalysis and Helius attribute memecoin sandwich vulnerability specifically to traders setting high slippage on illiquid pools. A single sandwich bot netted $13.43M in 30 days with 16 of its top-20 victim tokens being pump.fun tokens. Route privately and cap slippage.
- Your own position can be larger than the entire extractable value. The median Raydium soft rug is $2,832 and 25% are under $732. In pools that thin, backtested fills are fiction. Simulate execution against actual reserve depth, not against a mid price.
- Fake volume is cheap. Volume.li charges 0.212 ETH per $100,000 of generated volume (~0.6-0.8% of notional) and produced 43% of one token's Uniswap volume over 4 days. Any strategy keyed on volume or trade count is trivially and cheaply gamed against you.
- Wash-trading estimates differ by 500x between sources (21.4% of pump.fun transactions per MELT vs 0.035-0.046% of EVM DEX volume per Chainalysis). This is partly transactions-vs-USD-volume and partly a genuine chain difference. Do not port EVM manipulation priors to Solana launchpads.
- Social-presence features are the highest-lift cheap signal AND the most trivially gameable. An 8.94x Telegram lift will be arbitraged away as soon as it is widely known (and it is now published). Re-fit monthly and add liveness/quality checks (HTTP 200, channel member count, account age) rather than mere presence.
- Nearly every headline percentage circulating in crypto media traces back to a handful of Dune queries whose SQL you cannot inspect. The '25 wallets made over $10,000' figure in particular is inconsistent with every other source and should not be used. Prefer sources that publish methodology (Chainalysis, CoinGecko, the arXiv papers) over aggregator sites (coinlaw.io and similar returned 403 and could not be verified at all).
- Fee schedules changed repeatedly in 2025-2026 (PumpSwap launched at 0.25%, now runs a market-cap-scaled 1.25%→0.30%; creator fee sharing was overhauled Jan 9 2026). Fetch the live fee parameters from chain state rather than hardcoding, or your EV math silently drifts.

## Findings

### Pump.fun graduation rate is a regime variable ranging ~0.2% to ~6.7%, not a constant; treat it as a monthly-recalibrated parameter

Marino et al. (arXiv:2602.14860, Sept 1–Oct 1 2025): 4,338 of 655,770 tokens graduated = 0.63%. Kamat (arXiv:2607.02823, May 8–Jun 10 2026): 1,651 of 832,941 = 0.198% (Wilson 95% CI 0.189–0.208%) — BUT the author's own limitation note says the collector's effective coverage window was ~6 minutes not 24 hours, so this is a lower bound and NOT cleanly comparable to Marino's 0.63%. The Block (Feb 19 2026): weekly graduations 1.15% of launches, a 7-month high; historical peak ~2%, historical low ~0.5%. The Block (Jul 29 2026): after BOOST launched, graduation rate hit 6.7% on one Friday, 4.7% over the prior four days, 2.5% the week before, vs a June 2026 average of ~0.84%. arXiv:2512.11850 (Q4 2024): 'fewer than 2%'. crypto.news (Aug 2024): 33,683 of 1,883,578 = 1.79%. Solidus Labs (Jan 2024–Mar 2025): <100,000 of ~7,000,000 tokens ever held >$1,000 liquidity = ~1.4%.

**Actionable:** Do NOT hardcode a graduation rate. Compute a trailing 7-day and 30-day graduation rate from your own indexer every hour and expose it as `regime.grad_rate_7d`. Gate strategy aggressiveness on it: if grad_rate_7d < 0.5% run in defensive/observe mode; if > 3% (BOOST-like regime) allow larger size. Alert on any >2x week-over-week change — that means the launchpad changed mechanics and every prior-fit model is stale.

`confidence=high` · source: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823

### Graduation is arithmetically equivalent to roughly a 15x from block-0 price, so P(15x from launch) ≈ the graduation rate ≈ 1%

Marino et al. state the graduation threshold is 115 SOL total in the curve (30 virtual + 85 real) at ~$69,000 FDV. Pump.fun initial FDV is ~$4,000-5,500. 69,000 / 4,500 ≈ 15.3x. Therefore the fraction of launches that ever 15x from the first-block price is bounded above by (graduation rate) + (a small number of tokens that pumped hard on-curve but stalled before 85 real SOL). Given graduation rates of 0.198%–1.79% (normal regimes) and 4.7–6.7% (BOOST regime), P(≥15x from launch price) is ~0.2%–2% normally.

**Actionable:** Use grad_rate as your directly-measurable proxy for P(15x from launch). For a sniper that buys in the first few blocks, expected value per launch = grad_rate × (15x realized minus exit slippage) − (1 − grad_rate) × (loss on curve). At 1% grad rate and 15x, gross EV ≈ 0.15 − 0.99×L. With L=0.9 that is deeply negative. A block-0 sniper is only viable if you can (a) exit partially on the curve before graduation and (b) select launches with grad probability >5%.

`confidence=medium` · source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1

### The empirical post-migration outcome distribution: 60.26% of migrated Solana memecoins trade below 0.2x of migration price within 20 minutes; only 5.20% never trade below migration price

MemeTrans/MELT (arXiv:2602.13480), 41,470 memecoins that completed launchpad and migrated to DEX, Dec 1 2024 – Mar 1 2025, 200M+ transactions. min_price_ratio = min token price within 20 min post-migration / migration price. Distribution: [0.0–0.2) 24,988 (60.26%); [0.2–0.4) 5,265 (12.70%); [0.4–0.6) 3,315 (7.99%); [0.6–0.8) 2,991 (7.21%); [0.8–1.0) 2,756 (6.65%); ≥1.0 (never dipped) 2,155 (5.20%). Over 73% collapse below 0.4x of migration price within 20 minutes.

**Actionable:** This is your prior for any buy-at-migration strategy. A naive market buy at migration has a 60% chance of being down 80%+ within 20 minutes. Two direct implications: (1) never market-buy at the migration tick without a selection model; (2) if you do enter post-migration, place a hard stop at -25% to -35% and accept that you will be stopped out on the majority of entries; sizing must assume L (loss on losers) ≈ 0.30 with stops that actually fill, or ≈0.85 if they don't. Also: use the 5.20% 'never dipped' cohort as your positive label for a supervised model.

`confidence=high` · source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480v2

### Token mortality: 68.67% of pump.fun tokens record their last trade on launch day; 80.37% within one day; only 4.55% survive past 90 days

CoinGecko Research, 18.67 million tokens with nonzero trading, Jan 14 2024 – Jun 18 2026. Same day 12,825,175 (68.67%); 1 day 2,184,433 (11.70%); 2-3 days 770,249 (4.12%); 4-7 days 642,614 (3.44%); 8-14 days 460,697 (2.47%); 15-30 days 382,289 (2.05%); 31-60 days 337,931 (1.81%); 61-90 days 222,077 (1.19%); 90+ days 850,180 (4.55%). 'Lifespan' = calendar days between creation and last bonding-curve trade, so the 90+ bucket understates true survival for graduated tokens (post-graduation trades on PumpSwap/Raydium are not counted). Average launch rate >21,000 tokens/day. Corroborating: chainplay.gg reports ~10,417 launched vs ~9,912 defunct per 24h.

**Actionable:** Your universe decays with a half-life measured in hours. Any feature computed on a >6h window is computed on a survivorship-biased sample. Build your training labels with an explicit observation window (e.g. all launches in [T, T+24h]) and include the dead ones. Operationally: a position held >24h in a non-graduated token is almost certainly unexitable — enforce a max-hold timer.

`confidence=high` · source: https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/average-lifespan-of-pumpfun-tokens

### Time-to-graduation is measured in minutes, not hours — median ~4.4 minutes and ~457 bonding-curve swaps

Marino et al. (arXiv:2602.14860), Sept–Oct 2025: median time to graduation ~4.4 minutes from token creation; median ~457 bonding-curve swaps to graduation; distribution has a pronounced heavy tail. Kamat (arXiv:2607.02823), May–Jun 2026, within a 6-minute observation window: median 1.66 min, p90 2.80 min, max observed 5.98 min (truncated by the collector, so biased low). Galaxy Research (Oct 2025): median hold time for a Solana memecoin is 100 seconds, down from 300 seconds a year earlier.

**Actionable:** Your decision latency budget is seconds. A model that needs 5 minutes of trade history to score a launch will have missed the median graduation. Design two scoring tiers: T+0 to T+30s (creation metadata, dev wallet history, socials, first-buyer identity) and T+30s to T+180s (curve velocity, unique buyer count, bundle detection). Set your max-hold default to ~100-300 seconds to match the market's actual holding period; anything longer is a deliberate deviation from the crowd that needs its own justification.

`confidence=high` · source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1

### 98.6% of pump.fun tokens and 93% of Raydium pools show rug / pump-and-dump patterns; median soft rug size is $2,832

Solidus Labs 2025 Rug Pull Report, ~7 million pump.fun tokens Jan 2024 – Mar 2025: 98.6% flagged as rug pulls or manipulative schemes; fewer than ~100,000 (≈1.4%) ever maintained >$1,000 liquidity. Of 388,000 Raydium liquidity pools examined, 93% showed soft-rug patterns (developer abruptly withdrawing funds). 25% of Raydium rugs involved less than $732; median rug $2,832; largest identified $1.9M (MToken). Pump.fun publicly disputed the methodology (CoinDesk, May 7 2025), arguing that a $1,000-liquidity floor conflates 'failed/abandoned' with 'fraudulent'.

**Actionable:** Adopt the $1,000-liquidity floor as a hard universe filter: never trade a pool with < $1,000 (≈5 SOL) of quote-side liquidity — it is definitionally in the 98.6% bucket. Also note the median rug is tiny ($2,832), which means your own position size is often comparable to the entire extractable value; if your intended position is >2-3% of pool depth you are the exit liquidity. Cap position at a fixed fraction of quote reserves (suggest ≤1%).

`confidence=medium` · source: https://news.bitcoin.com/report-exposes-98-6-of-solana-meme-coins-on-pump-fun-as-fraudulent/

### 92.22% of pump.fun tokens with ≥30 swaps exhibit at least one statistically detected dump event

Marino et al. (arXiv:2602.14860): of 184,282 tokens with ≥30 swaps, 169,938 (92.22%) show at least one dump event under a Shewhart control-chart detector with a k=4 sigma threshold. The distribution of dump counts is right-skewed — most tokens have zero or few, a minority show repeated episodes.

**Actionable:** Implement the Shewhart k=4σ control chart on the signed SOL flow / price return series as a real-time exit trigger. Compute a rolling mean and sigma over the last N swaps (N≈30) and exit immediately on a −4σ excursion. This is a published, parameterized detector — use k=4 as the default and tune on your own data. Also use 'number of prior dump events' as a per-token risk feature.

`confidence=high` · source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1

### Coordinated bundling is pervasive: 28.13% of holders belong to bundles controlling 36.5% of total supply; high-risk tokens show +24pp top-10 concentration growth and +17pp first-10-buyer share
